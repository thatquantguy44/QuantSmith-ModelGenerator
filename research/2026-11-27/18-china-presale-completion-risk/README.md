## 18 — China Developer Presale Cash-Conversion and Completion Risk
**B — Domestic/global markets | China property developers and Asian credit | 88/100 | Medium-high | Conditional on data, capacity and validation; synthetic prototype feasible**
**Problem and decision:** Estimate completion funding gap using presales versus construction progress to support credit risk monitoring. Existing comparator (leverage and sales growth hazard) may fail under national aggregate cannot be attributed to specific developers; offshore/onshore exposure and escrow uncertain.
**H1 (proposed):** Presale funding-gap features improve next-two-quarter distress Brier score 10% beyond leverage and sales growth.
**Mathematics:** G_t=max(0,c·A_unfinished−cash_eligible−ρ·presale_receipts); c cost per sqm, A unfinished area, ρ legally usable receipt fraction; aggregate proxy separate from issuer model.
**Data/grain:** developer × reporting quarter / national monthly release; sales floor area, deposits/advance receipts, completed/started floor area, issuer cash and debt, escrow restrictions. **Clock:** first NBS report and issuer filing dissemination, convert cumulative series only using same vintage.
**Baseline → model → advanced:** leverage and sales growth hazard → stock-flow gap and competing-risk survival → hierarchical partially identified funding gaps with escrow scenarios.
**Evaluation:** distress Brier, calibration, completion error, uncertainty width. **Go/no-go:** ≥10% Brier improvement; no future completion data; abstain for unidentified issuer cash availability. **Negative tests:** cumulative reset January, revised comparable basis, negative cash, delisted issuers.
**Sources:** [NBS real estate development January–April 2026 (19 May 2026)](https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202605/t20260519_1963758.html); [SEC EDGAR API documentation (6 June 2024)](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces). Licensed: licensed Chinese/Asian bond prices, developer reports and corporate actions. Permissioned: approved credit exposure and escrow legal review.
**Illustrative 24-month scenario (USD thousands; not empirical ROI):** assumed total build/data/maintenance cost C=$165k; annual steady-state net operating benefit before project cost B=$120k; launch lag 7 months, no benefit during lag. Capacity assumption: $100m credit risk sleeve × 12 bps annual avoided-loss scenario=$120k/year, not observed default savings. Benefit over 24 months = B×(24−lag)/12=$170.0k; ROI=(benefit−C)/C=+3.0%. Half/base/1.5× benefit sensitivity -48.5%/+3.0%/+54.5%; zero benefit −100%; break-even annual benefit $116.5k. No actual adoption, market capacity, realized P&L or cash savings established; validate opportunity overlap and costs before business case.
**Publication potential:** Partial identification of construction funding stress. **Novelty:** Not sovereign DSA or US CMBS refinance: developer presale escrow-to-completion stock-flow. **Tools proposed:** lifelines,pymc,polars.
