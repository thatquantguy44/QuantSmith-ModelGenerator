## 08 — Marginal Initial-Margin Attribution for Repo Netting Sets
**A — Securities finance | US cleared repo / approved global netting sets | 89/100 | High | Conditional on data, capacity and validation; synthetic prototype feasible**
**Problem and decision:** Assign portfolio margin costs to strategies without treating gross exposure as actual marginal burden. Existing comparator (pro-rata gross notional allocation) may fail under black-box margin nonlinearities, allocation instability; hypothetical allocations not actual margin calls.
**H1 (proposed):** Coalition attribution reconciles total modeled margin and reduces misranking versus notional allocation on held-out trade additions.
**Mathematics:** φ_i=E_π[M(S_π∪{i})−M(S_π)], Σ_i φ_i=M(N)−M(∅); M approved margin calculator, S predecessor set. Negative contributions allowed but no cross-netting-set credit.
**Data/grain:** legal netting set × trade × approved portfolio snapshot; trade exposures, margin calculator/version, collateral requirements, legal partitions, realized margin changes. **Clock:** snapshot and calculator version known at allocation; never use future netting trades.
**Baseline → model → advanced:** pro-rata gross notional allocation → sampled Shapley attribution with reconciliation → stratified permutation/control variate plus incremental risk budget.
**Evaluation:** reconciliation error, holdout marginal-cost error, allocation stability, compute time. **Go/no-go:** relative error ≤1% reconciliation and ≥10% marginal MAE reduction; no illegal netting. **Negative tests:** empty coalition; hedged negative marginal; isolated netting sets; order invariance.
**Sources:** [DTCC rules and procedures](https://www.dtcc.com/legal/rules-and-procedures); [Basel CRE22 historical version](https://www.bis.org/committees/bcbs/basel-framework/standard/cre/22/inforce/2022-01-01/published/2019-12-15). Licensed: licensed pricing/risk engine inputs. Permissioned: approved margin calculator and netting-set snapshots.
**Illustrative 24-month scenario (USD thousands; not empirical ROI):** assumed total build/data/maintenance cost C=$175k; annual steady-state net operating benefit before project cost B=$150k; launch lag 7 months, no benefit during lag. Capacity assumption: $300m modeled margin-sensitive inventory equivalent × 5 bps estimated allocation efficiency = $150k/year; attribution itself books no savings. Benefit over 24 months = B×(24−lag)/12=$212.5k; ROI=(benefit−C)/C=+21.4%. Half/base/1.5× benefit sensitivity -39.3%/+21.4%/+82.1%; zero benefit −100%; break-even annual benefit $123.5k. No actual adoption, market capacity, realized P&L or cash savings established; validate opportunity overlap and costs before business case.
**Publication potential:** Cooperative game theory and risk attribution. **Novelty:** Unlike clearing access path, compression, porting or default-fund tail allocation: allocate existing netting-set initial margin for decision support. **Tools proposed:** numpy,joblib,scipy.
