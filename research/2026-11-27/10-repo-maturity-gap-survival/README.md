## 10 — Repo Maturity Mismatch Early-Warning via Survival Cash Ladders
**A — Securities finance | US and European matched-book secured funding | 87/100 | Medium-high | Conditional on data, capacity and validation; synthetic prototype feasible**
**Problem and decision:** Forecast replacement funding gaps from conditional rollover survival rather than static maturity mismatch. Existing comparator (contractual maturity ladder with fixed stress rollover percentage) may fail under censoring of renewals and structural shifts; predicted rollover not assured liquidity.
**H1 (proposed):** Survival-weighted cash ladders improve next-five-day liquidity shortfall Brier score by 10% versus contractual-only ladders.
**Mathematics:** G_h=Σ_i N_i 1{m_i≤h}(1−p_i(h))−Σ_j R_j(h); N due liabilities, m maturity, p conditional rollover probability, R confirmed incoming cash; committed resources alone count toward hard limits.
**Data/grain:** repo trade × maturity bucket × daily snapshot; maturity dates, historical renewals/nonrenewals, counterparty segment, rates, confirmed inflows and cutoff. **Clock:** decision before maturity; only renewals confirmed by snapshot; outcomes as labels.
**Baseline → model → advanced:** contractual maturity ladder with fixed stress rollover percentage → competing-risk survival and calibrated gap scenarios → hierarchical regime-dependent survival with correlated nonrenewal.
**Evaluation:** Brier score, cash-gap quantile coverage, warning lead time, reserve cost. **Go/no-go:** ≥10% Brier improvement and adequate 95% gap interval coverage; no uncommitted cash counted as guaranteed. **Negative tests:** no renewals in stress, maturity concentration, missing confirmations.
**Sources:** [OFR repo release](https://www.financialresearch.gov/short-term-funding-monitor/datasets/repo/); [New York Fed reference rates](https://www.newyorkfed.org/markets/reference-rates); [ESMA EU SFT market overview (9 April 2024)](https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esma-publishes-first-overview-eu-securities-financing-transactions-markets). Licensed: licensed repo term and market stress history. Permissioned: approved contractual maturity and rollover records.
**Illustrative 24-month scenario (USD thousands; not empirical ROI):** assumed total build/data/maintenance cost C=$145k; annual steady-state net operating benefit before project cost B=$120k; launch lag 6 months, no benefit during lag. Capacity assumption: $4m avoided excess average reserve × 3% net carrying cost=$120k/year; no relaxation of required liquidity. Benefit over 24 months = B×(24−lag)/12=$180.0k; ROI=(benefit−C)/C=+24.1%. Half/base/1.5× benefit sensitivity -37.9%/+24.1%/+86.2%; zero benefit −100%; break-even annual benefit $96.7k. No actual adoption, market capacity, realized P&L or cash savings established; validate opportunity overlap and costs before business case.
**Publication potential:** Survival analysis for matched-book cash maturity gaps. **Novelty:** Not term curve dislocation or LCR/NSFR shadow costs: forecast actual renewal failure and cash gap over horizon. **Tools proposed:** lifelines,statsmodels,pandas.
