## 20 — Global Pension Contribution-Aware Rebalancing with Trading Frictions
**B — Domestic/global markets | US, UK, Canada and Netherlands institutional allocation | 86/100 | Medium | Conditional on data, capacity and validation; synthetic prototype feasible**
**Problem and decision:** Use forecast contributions/benefits to rebalance policy weights without unnecessary turnover. Existing comparator (calendar rebalancing with identical cashflow forecast and policy constraints) may fail under OECD aggregates cannot reveal real plan trades; benefit schedules private.
**H1 (proposed):** Cashflow-aware no-trade bands lower cost 10% versus calendar rebalancing at matched policy tracking risk.
**Mathematics:** min_w (w−w*)'Σ(w−w*)+λΣ c_i|trade_i|; cash balance Σtrade_i=C_t−B_t; long-only policy bounds; w weights, Σ return covariance, C/B cash contributions/benefits, c costs.
**Data/grain:** plan type × asset class × month / public annual country aggregates; policy weights, cash contributions/benefits, portfolio NAV, returns and executable costs. **Clock:** only annual OECD releases for long-run calibration; approved plan cashflows known before month-end decisions.
**Baseline → model → advanced:** calendar rebalancing with identical cashflow forecast and policy constraints → convex no-trade-band optimizer → multi-period stochastic control using uncertain benefit payments.
**Evaluation:** net transaction costs, tracking error, liquidity shortfalls, turnover. **Go/no-go:** ≥10% cost reduction at noninferior tracking risk and zero cash deficits. **Negative tests:** cash outflow shock, policy band breach, zero liquidity, stale NAV.
**Sources:** [OECD Pension Markets in Focus 2025 (12 November 2025)](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/pension-markets-in-focus-2025_b095d0a0-en.html). Licensed: licensed index returns and execution cost curves. Permissioned: approved plan policy and cashflow schedules.
**Illustrative 24-month scenario (USD thousands; not empirical ROI):** assumed total build/data/maintenance cost C=$110k; annual steady-state net operating benefit before project cost B=$100k; launch lag 5 months, no benefit during lag. Capacity assumption: $500m illustrative portfolio × 2 bps annual net turnover savings=$100k/year; no trading strategy alpha. Benefit over 24 months = B×(24−lag)/12=$158.3k; ROI=(benefit−C)/C=+43.9%. Half/base/1.5× benefit sensitivity -28.0%/+43.9%/+115.9%; zero benefit −100%; break-even annual benefit $69.5k. No actual adoption, market capacity, realized P&L or cash savings established; validate opportunity overlap and costs before business case.
**Publication potential:** Transaction-cost control under institutional contribution/benefit cash flows. **Novelty:** Not global covariance-break risk budget or currency hedge: exogenous pension cashflow timing with policy bands. **Tools proposed:** cvxpy,numpy,pandas.
