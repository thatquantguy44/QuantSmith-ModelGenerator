# Research brief — Forward Borrow Capacity Reservation for Committed Term Loans

## 03 — Forward Borrow Capacity Reservation for Committed Term Loans
**A — Securities finance | US and European equity stock loan | 94/100 | High | Conditional on data, capacity and validation; synthetic prototype feasible**
**Problem and decision:** Decide how much inventory to reserve against future firm lending commitments versus spot availability. Existing comparator (fixed fraction of expected term draws, subject to same owner rights) may fail under historical accepted demand is selected; inventory cannot be promised against unconfirmed availability.
**H1 (proposed):** Quantile reservation cuts shortage and lost spot-fee costs by 5% versus fixed reserve percentages.
**Mathematics:** min_r E[c_s(r−D)^+ + c_f(D−r)^+] with 0≤r≤I−other commitments; r reserved shares, D eligible future term draw, c_s forgone spot dollars/share, c_f replacement/penalty dollars/share.
**Data/grain:** security × owner mandate × commitment vintage; signed term commitments, available shares, draw schedules, spot fees, cancellations, replacement cost. **Clock:** at commitment acceptance or daily reserve review; only confirmed mandates and availability.
**Baseline → model → advanced:** fixed fraction of expected term draws, subject to same owner rights → newsvendor quantile with censored draws and lead times → multi-period distributionally robust reservation with scenario owner withdrawals.
**Evaluation:** shortage shares, realized reservation cost, service coverage, revenue. **Go/no-go:** ≥5% lower total cost, no worse fulfillment rate, zero double booking. **Negative tests:** concurrent commitment consumes same shares; owner withdrawal; zero available shares.
**Sources:** [ESMA EU SFT market overview (9 April 2024)](https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esma-publishes-first-overview-eu-securities-financing-transactions-markets); [FINRA API documentation](https://developer.finra.org/docs). Licensed: licensed fee/availability snapshots. Permissioned: approved signed commitments and draw histories.
**Illustrative 24-month scenario (USD thousands; not empirical ROI):** assumed total build/data/maintenance cost C=$180k; annual steady-state net operating benefit before project cost B=$200k; launch lag 6 months, no benefit during lag. Capacity assumption: $250m eligible inventory × 8 bp/year avoided shortage/opportunity costs = $200k/year, after modeled variable frictions. Benefit over 24 months = B×(24−lag)/12=$300.0k; ROI=(benefit−C)/C=+66.7%. Half/base/1.5× benefit sensitivity -16.7%/+66.7%/+150.0%; zero benefit −100%; break-even annual benefit $120.0k. No actual adoption, market capacity, realized P&L or cash savings established; validate opportunity overlap and costs before business case.
**Publication potential:** Capacity reservation under competing lending demand. **Novelty:** Different from futures delivery inventory reservation: target contractual term-loan draw rights; different from early recall decisions. **Tools proposed:** scipy,statsmodels,ortools.
