# ROADMAP — Forward Borrow Capacity Reservation for Committed Term Loans
**QMG-20261127-03 | A — Securities finance | US and European equity stock loan | proposal only**
**Weeks 1–2:** Validate signed term commitments, available shares, draw schedules, spot fees, cancellations, replacement cost, at commitment acceptance or daily reserve review; only confirmed mandates and availability, permissions and historical universe. Stop without defensible data or baseline. **Weeks 3–4:** Construct fixtures for concurrent commitment consumes same shares; owner withdrawal; zero available shares; implement fixed fraction of expected term draws, subject to same owner rights, pass AC-001/002. **Weeks 5–8:** Prototype newsvendor quantile with censored draws and lead times, tune on validation, compare multi-period distributionally robust reservation with scenario owner withdrawals if baseline remains beatable. Freeze holdout. **Weeks 9–12:** Evaluate shortage shares, realized reservation cost, service coverage, revenue; stop unless ≥5% lower total cost, no worse fulfillment rate, zero double booking. Verify $250m eligible inventory × 8 bp/year avoided shortage/opportunity costs = $200k/year, after modeled variable frictions. against actual eligible opportunities and costs. **Subsequent:** 30-day or one outcome cycle (whichever longer) read-only shadow, independent review, no production until authorization. Timeline indicative; dependencies on data access and legal review.
