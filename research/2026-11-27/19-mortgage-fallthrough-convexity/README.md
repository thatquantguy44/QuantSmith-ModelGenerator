## 19 — US Mortgage Origination Fall-Through and Rate-Lock Convexity
**B — Domestic/global markets | US residential mortgage origination and rates | 87/100 | High | Conditional on data, capacity and validation; synthetic prototype feasible**
**Problem and decision:** Improve pipeline hedge sizing by estimating application fallout sensitivity to rate moves. Existing comparator (fixed pull-through by stage and loan type) may fail under HMDA lacks lock-level timestamps; public model cannot establish live hedge efficacy.
**H1 (proposed):** Competing-risk fallout forecasts reduce hedge error 10% against fixed pull-through assumptions on approved lock-level histories.
**Mathematics:** H_t=Σ_i N_i p_i(t) DV01_i; choose hedge z minimizing E[(H_tΔy+zΔF)^2]+cost(z), p pull-through, DV01 dollar per bp; z bounded by mandate.
**Data/grain:** application/lock × decision date; public HMDA annual cohort only; loan outcomes, rates, application attributes, actual lock time, lock rate/expiry, hedge prices. **Clock:** public HMDA only after annual release; private locks timestamped before hedge; no demographic targeting.
**Baseline → model → advanced:** fixed pull-through by stage and loan type → competing-risk duration model with rate gap → joint hazard and convexity hedge with correlated fallout scenarios.
**Evaluation:** hedge error, pull-through calibration, bias, cost and residual duration. **Go/no-go:** ≥10% hedge RMSE reduction at equal costs; no use of protected attributes for decisions. **Negative tests:** withdrawals, lock extension, refi regime, future rate and applicant privacy.
**Sources:** [CFPB 2025 HMDA release (31 March 2026)](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/about-us/newsroom/2025-hmda-data-on-mortgage-lending-now-available/); [Freddie Mac PMMS](https://www.freddiemac.com/pmms). Licensed: licensed TBA/rates quotes and mortgage pipeline benchmarks. Permissioned: approved de-identified lock-level history, no raw applicant data publication.
**Illustrative 24-month scenario (USD thousands; not empirical ROI):** assumed total build/data/maintenance cost C=$170k; annual steady-state net operating benefit before project cost B=$100k; launch lag 8 months, no benefit during lag. Capacity assumption: $100m average rate-locked pipeline × 10 bps hypothetical annual hedge improvement=$100k/year. Benefit over 24 months = B×(24−lag)/12=$133.3k; ROI=(benefit−C)/C=-21.6%. Half/base/1.5× benefit sensitivity -60.8%/-21.6%/+17.6%; zero benefit −100%; break-even annual benefit $127.5k. No actual adoption, market capacity, realized P&L or cash savings established; validate opportunity overlap and costs before business case.
**Publication potential:** Survival models for mortgage origination convexity. **Novelty:** Not agency MBS pool delivery dollar-roll or Canadian mortgage renewal: origination pipeline fallout and hedge DV01. **Tools proposed:** lifelines,statsmodels,numpy.
