## 13 — Swiss Franc Conditional Safe-Haven Hedge Reliability
**B — Domestic/global markets | Switzerland, euro area and global risk portfolios | 93/100 | Medium-high | Conditional on data, capacity and validation; synthetic prototype feasible**
**Problem and decision:** Size CHF risk-off hedges conditional on funding and volatility regimes, not intervention dates. Existing comparator (fixed CHF allocation with equal carry budget) may fail under safe-haven relation unstable; overlapping global closes and intervention confounding.
**H1 (proposed):** Conditional hedge reduces global portfolio expected shortfall 10% relative to fixed CHF hedge at equal carry budget.
**Mathematics:** min_z ES_95%(−(r_p+z r_CHF))+cost(z); z bounded, |Δz| turnover constrained, returns in common currency; state estimated only from lagged information.
**Data/grain:** currency pair × day × decision timestamp; CHF exchange rates, policy rates, risk factors, global equity returns, executable FX hedge quotes. **Clock:** daily decisions after all required markets close, execute next quote; no revised intervention data.
**Baseline → model → advanced:** fixed CHF allocation with equal carry budget → threshold quantile regression on risk-state proxies → hidden Markov state model with robust scenario hedge.
**Evaluation:** expected shortfall, carry, hedge hit rate, turnover. **Go/no-go:** ≥10% ES improvement at matched carry and acceptable return sacrifice; robust to crisis leave-one-out. **Negative tests:** crisis placebo, asynchronous close, stale rate, one-event dominance.
**Sources:** [SNB rates and FX data](https://data.snb.ch/de/topics/ziredev); [OECD Pension Markets in Focus 2025 (12 November 2025)](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/pension-markets-in-focus-2025_b095d0a0-en.html). Licensed: licensed FX forward curves and total return indexes. Permissioned: approved portfolio beta and currency hedge limits.
**Illustrative 24-month scenario (USD thousands; not empirical ROI):** assumed total build/data/maintenance cost C=$145k; annual steady-state net operating benefit before project cost B=$80k; launch lag 6 months, no benefit during lag. Capacity assumption: $200m risk budget × 4 bps annual hypothetical avoided hedging cost=$80k/year; tail-risk reduction not cash revenue unless monetized. Benefit over 24 months = B×(24−lag)/12=$120.0k; ROI=(benefit−C)/C=-17.2%. Half/base/1.5× benefit sensitivity -58.6%/-17.2%/+24.1%; zero benefit −100%; break-even annual benefit $96.7k. No actual adoption, market capacity, realized P&L or cash savings established; validate opportunity overlap and costs before business case.
**Publication potential:** State-dependent hedge reliability and tail dependence. **Novelty:** Not Japan intervention event study or dynamic global equity currency hedging: conditional CHF tail hedge at fixed carry. **Tools proposed:** statsmodels,hmmlearn,cvxpy.
