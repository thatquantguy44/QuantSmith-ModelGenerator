## 04 — Benchmark Reset Convention Basis in Securities Loan Rebates
**A — Securities finance | USD and GBP cash-collateral lending | 93/100 | Medium | High for deterministic/public-data prototype; market claims blocked**
**Problem and decision:** Quantify mismatch between contractually specified rate resets and cash-investment accruals before renewals. Existing comparator (same-day benchmark with standard day count) may fail under ambiguous fallback language and publication lag; legacy terms not inferable from index name.
**H1 (proposed):** Contract-clock replication explains at least 95% of synthetic/confirmed rebate mismatch and reduces unexplained realized basis versus same-day-rate treatment.
**Mathematics:** B=Σ_d N_d Δ_d [r_asset(d)−r_rebate(g(d))−s_d]; N notional, Δ contractual year fraction, g maps accrual date to authorized fixing, s fees; respect lookback, holiday and rounding.
**Data/grain:** contract × accrual date × fixing vintage; rate benchmark vintages, executed convention text, spread, calendars, balance and asset accruals. **Clock:** fixings available only after publication; project unknown future rates as scenarios, never realized inputs.
**Baseline → model → advanced:** same-day benchmark with standard day count → deterministic reset/compounding convention engine → scenario curve and hedge sensitivity under competing reset clocks.
**Evaluation:** reconciliation USD, residual basis, projected mismatch, fixture accuracy. **Go/no-go:** 100% golden convention fixtures and ≥95% explained confirmed discrepancies; no unexplained sign errors. **Negative tests:** weekend accrual, leap day, missing fixing, negative rate, different day counts.
**Sources:** [New York Fed reference rates](https://www.newyorkfed.org/markets/reference-rates); [Bank of England SONIA methodology](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/markets/sonia%20benchmark/sonia%20key%20features%20and%20policies). Licensed: licensed forward curves and funding quotes. Permissioned: approved executed convention text and cash accrual ledger.
**Illustrative 24-month scenario (USD thousands; not empirical ROI):** assumed total build/data/maintenance cost C=$100k; annual steady-state net operating benefit before project cost B=$150k; launch lag 4 months, no benefit during lag. Capacity assumption: $500m average balance × 3 bps annual preventable basis = $150k/year; no speculative trading profits. Benefit over 24 months = B×(24−lag)/12=$250.0k; ROI=(benefit−C)/C=+150.0%. Half/base/1.5× benefit sensitivity +25.0%/+150.0%/+275.0%; zero benefit −100%; break-even annual benefit $60.0k. No actual adoption, market capacity, realized P&L or cash savings established; validate opportunity overlap and costs before business case.
**Publication potential:** Reproducible multi-calendar accrual microstructure. **Novelty:** Unlike rebate floor optionality or retroactive rate amendments, compare accrual clocks and basis of authorized benchmarks. **Tools proposed:** decimal,pandas,QuantLib.
