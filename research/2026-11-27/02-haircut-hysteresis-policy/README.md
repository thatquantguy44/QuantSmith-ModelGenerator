## 02 — Countercyclical Repo Haircut Adjustment with Hysteresis
**A — Securities finance | US/EU non-cleared secured funding | 95/100 | High | Conditional on data, capacity and validation; synthetic prototype feasible**
**Problem and decision:** Choose an approved haircut adjustment rule that limits cliff effects while protecting coverage. Existing comparator (rolling volatility haircut with same mandatory floors) may fail under selection by counterparty and tail scarcity; slower hikes may worsen credit losses.
**H1 (proposed):** Hysteresis reduces peak incremental margin liquidity demand by 10% versus rolling-volatility haircuts with no deterioration in closeout tail loss.
**Mathematics:** h_t=max(h_floor,min(h_cap,h_{t−1}+clip(g(z_t)−h_{t−1},−δ_down,δ_up))); z stress, δ approved adjustment caps; compare L=max(E−V_liq,0). Mandatory increases override rate caps.
**Data/grain:** collateral class × legal agreement × risk day; haircut vintages, eligible collateral marks, stress indicators, margin calls and realized liquidation outcomes. **Clock:** previously published volatility/rates, signed terms before risk decision.
**Baseline → model → advanced:** rolling volatility haircut with same mandatory floors → hysteretic threshold controller → robust stochastic policy with joint liquidation and funding shocks.
**Evaluation:** p99 incremental margin call, expected shortfall of closeout, floor breaches. **Go/no-go:** ≥10% reduction in p99 call; upper 95% bound for incremental ES≤approved tolerance; zero floor violations. **Negative tests:** mandatory floor jump; fast crash; prohibited haircut decrease.
**Sources:** [FSB haircut framework, 7 September 2020](https://www.fsb.org/2020/09/regulatory-framework-for-haircuts-on-non-centrally-cleared-securities-financing-transactions-5/); [BIS Working Paper 1121, Margins, debt capacity, and systemic risk (7 September 2023)](https://www.bis.org/publications/working-paper-1121-margins-debt-capacity-and-systemic-risk); [Basel CRE22 historical version](https://www.bis.org/committees/bcbs/basel-framework/standard/cre/22/inforce/2022-01-01/published/2019-12-15). Licensed: licensed security prices, liquidation depth and haircut history. Permissioned: approved haircut policy and closeout event data.
**Illustrative 24-month scenario (USD thousands; not empirical ROI):** assumed total build/data/maintenance cost C=$190k; annual steady-state net operating benefit before project cost B=$120k; launch lag 8 months, no benefit during lag. Capacity assumption: Illustrative $400m affected collateral; $120k annual avoided reserve carrying cost at assumed 3% on $4m lower reserve; no credit loss improvement assumed. Benefit over 24 months = B×(24−lag)/12=$160.0k; ROI=(benefit−C)/C=-15.8%. Half/base/1.5× benefit sensitivity -57.9%/-15.8%/+26.3%; zero benefit −100%; break-even annual benefit $142.5k. No actual adoption, market capacity, realized P&L or cash savings established; validate opportunity overlap and costs before business case.
**Publication potential:** Control theory for margin procyclicality and stress resilience. **Novelty:** Not static collateral cluster haircuts: target temporal adjustment path with hard credit safeguards. **Tools proposed:** numpy,scipy,cvxpy.
