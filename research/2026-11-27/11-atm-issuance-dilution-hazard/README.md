## 11 — US Small-Cap ATM Equity Issuance Dilution Hazard
**B — Domestic/global markets | US small-cap equities | 96/100 | Medium | Conditional on data, capacity and validation; synthetic prototype feasible**
**Problem and decision:** Estimate dilution exposure and adjust research risk after publicly disclosed ATM programs. Existing comparator (cash-runway and market-cap logistic hazard) may fail under ATM authorization is not execution; reverse splits, delayed quarterly reports and failed issuers.
**H1 (proposed):** Program-level issuance hazard improves next-quarter dilution prediction by 10% versus cash-runway and size baseline.
**Mathematics:** P(Δshares>q|X)=logit^-1(βX), exposure D=issued_shares/pre_issue_shares; q=5% preregistered; only public program size and remaining disclosed capacity; no unreported actual sales.
**Data/grain:** issuer CIK × accession × trading day; 424B prospectuses, 8-K/10-Q share counts, cash burn, issuance disclosures, delisting-adjusted returns. **Clock:** first dissemination of each accession, next tradable session; quarter issuance outcomes after filings.
**Baseline → model → advanced:** cash-runway and market-cap logistic hazard → survival model with disclosed program capacity and competing financing events → hierarchical joint issuance-size and event-return model.
**Evaluation:** Brier, dilution MAE, event return attribution, false-alert rate. **Go/no-go:** ≥10% Brier improvement; precision improvement at fixed recall; no surviving-only universe. **Negative tests:** authorization without issuance; reverse split; delisting; duplicate supplement.
**Sources:** [SEC EDGAR API documentation (6 June 2024)](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces); [SEC financial statement and notes data](https://www.sec.gov/data-research/sec-markets-data/financial-statement-notes-data-sets). Licensed: point-in-time prices, split/delisting returns and issuance vendor. Permissioned: approved portfolio exposure for risk translation.
**Illustrative 24-month scenario (USD thousands; not empirical ROI):** assumed total build/data/maintenance cost C=$130k; annual steady-state net operating benefit before project cost B=$150k; launch lag 5 months, no benefit during lag. Capacity assumption: $100m risk sleeve × 15 bps annual net avoided loss scenario=$150k/year; no alpha assumed measured. Benefit over 24 months = B×(24−lag)/12=$237.5k; ROI=(benefit−C)/C=+82.7%. Half/base/1.5× benefit sensitivity -8.7%/+82.7%/+174.0%; zero benefit −100%; break-even annual benefit $82.1k. No actual adoption, market capacity, realized P&L or cash savings established; validate opportunity overlap and costs before business case.
**Publication potential:** Corporate financing hazard and disclosure extraction. **Novelty:** Different from customer concentration, buyback completion and FDA catalysts: forward issuance dilution. **Tools proposed:** lxml,polars,lifelines.
