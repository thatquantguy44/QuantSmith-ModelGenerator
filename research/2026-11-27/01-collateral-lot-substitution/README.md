## 01 — Collateral Substitution with Discrete Lot Opportunity Costs
**A — Securities finance | US Treasury and European sovereign repo | 97/100 | High | Conditional on data, capacity and validation; synthetic prototype feasible**
**Problem and decision:** Release high-opportunity-value pledged lots without increasing coverage breaches. Existing comparator (cheapest eligible collateral first with same lot and transfer constraints) may fail under solver can suggest illegal or unsettled substitution if pending collateral is counted.
**H1 (proposed):** A lot-aware substitution policy cuts total collateral carry and transfer costs by at least 5% against cheapest-eligible-first, with no extra coverage breaches.
**Mathematics:** min Σ_i,j c_i x_ij + Σ_i,j k_ij |x_ij−x0_ij|; x integer lots; Σ_i v_i(1−h_ij)x_ij≥R_j; Σ_j x_ij≤I_i. i lot, j obligation, c carry, k transfer, v lot value, h haircut, R required coverage, I inventory.
**Data/grain:** lot × obligation × snapshot; lot size, live eligibility, haircut, encumbrance, transfer fees, cutoffs, available inventory and replacement opportunity cost. **Clock:** pre-substitution snapshot; confirm replacement settlement before release; no end-day inventory.
**Baseline → model → advanced:** cheapest eligible collateral first with same lot and transfer constraints → integer min-cost assignment with opportunity-cost duals → rolling stochastic mixed-integer control with replacement failure recourse.
**Evaluation:** net USD carry/transfer cost, excess collateral, breach count, solve time. **Go/no-go:** ≥5% lower matched net cost; zero incremental eligibility or coverage violations; p95 solve time within agreed cutoffs. **Negative tests:** one scarce lot assigned twice; replacement not final; haircut shock; infeasible obligation.
**Sources:** [DTCC rules and procedures](https://www.dtcc.com/legal/rules-and-procedures); [OFR repo release](https://www.financialresearch.gov/short-term-funding-monitor/datasets/repo/). Licensed: security-level executable financing costs and reference data. Permissioned: approved collateral lots and eligibility agreements.
**Illustrative 24-month scenario (USD thousands; not empirical ROI):** assumed total build/data/maintenance cost C=$170k; annual steady-state net operating benefit before project cost B=$160k; launch lag 6 months, no benefit during lag. Capacity assumption: Assume $1bn average eligible inventory and 2 bps annual gross opportunity improvement, less $40k transfer overhead; $160k/year net before project costs. Benefit over 24 months = B×(24−lag)/12=$240.0k; ROI=(benefit−C)/C=+41.2%. Half/base/1.5× benefit sensitivity -29.4%/+41.2%/+111.8%; zero benefit −100%; break-even annual benefit $113.3k. No actual adoption, market capacity, realized P&L or cash savings established; validate opportunity overlap and costs before business case.
**Publication potential:** Discrete optimization under settlement finality and cost duals. **Novelty:** Unlike eligibility-cliff prepositioning or cash/noncash frontier, optimize existing eligible indivisible lots and turnover. **Tools proposed:** ortools,highspy,polars.
