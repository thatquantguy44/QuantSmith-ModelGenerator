Agent ID: collateral-lot-substitution-researcher. Mandate: Release high-opportunity-value pledged lots without increasing coverage breaches. Test A lot-aware substitution policy cuts total collateral carry and transfer costs by at least 5% against cheapest-eligible-first, with no extra coverage breaches.. Inputs: as_of, source manifest, lot × obligation × snapshot state, permitted constraints, baseline configuration, cost and holdout. Outputs: source rights and clock ledger, baseline/candidate results if executed, net USD carry/transfer cost, excess collateral, breach count, solve time, claim classifications, blockers and go/no-go. Agent may draft specifications and synthetic fixtures; only deterministic code calculates economics. Independent reviewer checks ≥5% lower matched net cost; zero incremental eligibility or coverage violations; p95 solve time within agreed cutoffs and one scarce lot assigned twice; replacement not final; haircut shock; infeasible obligation. Fail closed on solver can suggest illegal or unsettled substitution if pending collateral is counted, invalid source rights or missing costs. No trading, private disclosure, legal determinations or deployment. If data absent return BLOCKED, not simulated evidence.
