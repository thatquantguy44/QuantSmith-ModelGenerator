## 07 — Repo RFQ Disclosure-Width versus Execution Competition
**A — Securities finance | US and UK institutional repo | 90/100 | High | Conditional on data, capacity and validation; synthetic prototype feasible**
**Problem and decision:** Select an approved RFQ recipient count under information leakage and liquidity constraints. Existing comparator (fixed approved number of recipients) may fail under interference across simultaneous RFQs and confidential order exposure; observational comparisons biased.
**H1 (proposed):** A prespecified randomized recipient-width experiment lowers all-in execution costs versus fixed-width RFQs by 2% with no fill-rate deterioration.
**Mathematics:** min_n E[spread(n)×N×τ + impact(n)+message_cost(n)] s.t. n∈approved set, fill≥p_min; N USD, τ year fraction, costs dollars.
**Data/grain:** RFQ × recipient-count assignment × session; RFQ timestamps, eligible recipients, executable responses, trade cost, approved randomization assignment and later price drift. **Clock:** assign before outreach; only historical availability and quote freshness; observe outcomes later.
**Baseline → model → advanced:** fixed approved number of recipients → randomized experiment and heterogeneous treatment effects → constrained contextual bandit only after randomized support and approval.
**Evaluation:** all-in cost, fill probability, post-RFQ drift, coverage and latency. **Go/no-go:** ≥2% lower cost, positive paired confidence bound; noninferior fills; zero policy breaches. **Negative tests:** assignment imbalance; correlated RFQs; non-executable response; unsupported recipient.
**Sources:** [OFR repo release](https://www.financialresearch.gov/short-term-funding-monitor/datasets/repo/); [ESMA EU SFT market overview (9 April 2024)](https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esma-publishes-first-overview-eu-securities-financing-transactions-markets). Licensed: independent licensed repo marks and executable reference quotes. Permissioned: approved anonymized RFQ event records and consented experiment protocol.
**Illustrative 24-month scenario (USD thousands; not empirical ROI):** assumed total build/data/maintenance cost C=$210k; annual steady-state net operating benefit before project cost B=$150k; launch lag 9 months, no benefit during lag. Capacity assumption: $1bn annual average financed balance equivalent × 1.5 bps net savings = $150k/year; include leakage and message costs. Benefit over 24 months = B×(24−lag)/12=$187.5k; ROI=(benefit−C)/C=-10.7%. Half/base/1.5× benefit sensitivity -55.4%/-10.7%/+33.9%; zero benefit −100%; break-even annual benefit $168.0k. No actual adoption, market capacity, realized P&L or cash savings established; validate opportunity overlap and costs before business case.
**Publication potential:** Market microstructure and randomized information design. **Novelty:** Distinct from historical execution-shortfall attribution and structural auction pricing: experimentally vary disclosure breadth. **Tools proposed:** statsmodels,econml,numpy.
