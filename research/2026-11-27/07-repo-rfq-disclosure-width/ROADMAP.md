# ROADMAP — Repo RFQ Disclosure-Width versus Execution Competition
**QMG-20261127-07 | A — Securities finance | US and UK institutional repo | proposal only**
**Weeks 1–2:** Validate RFQ timestamps, eligible recipients, executable responses, trade cost, approved randomization assignment and later price drift, assign before outreach; only historical availability and quote freshness; observe outcomes later, permissions and historical universe. Stop without defensible data or baseline. **Weeks 3–4:** Construct fixtures for assignment imbalance; correlated RFQs; non-executable response; unsupported recipient; implement fixed approved number of recipients, pass AC-001/002. **Weeks 5–8:** Prototype randomized experiment and heterogeneous treatment effects, tune on validation, compare constrained contextual bandit only after randomized support and approval if baseline remains beatable. Freeze holdout. **Weeks 9–12:** Evaluate all-in cost, fill probability, post-RFQ drift, coverage and latency; stop unless ≥2% lower cost, positive paired confidence bound; noninferior fills; zero policy breaches. Verify $1bn annual average financed balance equivalent × 1.5 bps net savings = $150k/year; include leakage and message costs. against actual eligible opportunities and costs. **Subsequent:** 30-day or one outcome cycle (whichever longer) read-only shadow, independent review, no production until authorization. Timeline indicative; dependencies on data access and legal review.
