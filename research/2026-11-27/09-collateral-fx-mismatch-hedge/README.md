## 09 — Collateral FX Mismatch Hedge under Margin Revaluation
**A — Securities finance | USD/EUR/GBP securities finance | 88/100 | High | Conditional on data, capacity and validation; synthetic prototype feasible**
**Problem and decision:** Choose a permissible FX hedge size for existing collateral exposure in another currency. Existing comparator (static full currency hedge with same collateral and limits) may fail under hedges may not qualify as collateral or reduce regulatory haircut; joint FX/security tails unstable.
**H1 (proposed):** Tail-sensitive FX hedge reduces 10-day margin-call ES by 10% versus static full hedge net of hedge costs.
**Mathematics:** min_z ES_α([E−V·X+z(X−X0)]^+)+κ|z|; E domestic obligation, V foreign collateral units, X domestic FX, z short foreign units, κ cost; enforce limits and mandatory haircuts.
**Data/grain:** collateral basket × currency pair × margin day; contract currency, collateral value, FX quotes, hedge instruments, margin frequency, hedge liquidity and constraints. **Clock:** only FX quotes/eligibility known before margin and hedge cutoff; hedge at next executable quote.
**Baseline → model → advanced:** static full currency hedge with same collateral and limits → scenario expected-shortfall hedge sizing → multi-period robust hedge with FX/collateral joint tails.
**Evaluation:** margin call ES, hedge slippage, capital/FX residual, hard constraints. **Go/no-go:** ≥10% lower ES with total costs reported, no legal/floor violations. **Negative tests:** FX gap, stale collateral quote, hedge illiquidity, mandatory haircut unchanged.
**Sources:** [Basel CRE22 historical version](https://www.bis.org/committees/bcbs/basel-framework/standard/cre/22/inforce/2022-01-01/published/2019-12-15); [SNB rates and FX data](https://data.snb.ch/de/topics/ziredev); [FSB haircut framework, 7 September 2020](https://www.fsb.org/2020/09/regulatory-framework-for-haircuts-on-non-centrally-cleared-securities-financing-transactions-5/). Licensed: licensed synchronized FX forwards and collateral price histories. Permissioned: authorized collateral exposures and hedge policy.
**Illustrative 24-month scenario (USD thousands; not empirical ROI):** assumed total build/data/maintenance cost C=$180k; annual steady-state net operating benefit before project cost B=$120k; launch lag 8 months, no benefit during lag. Capacity assumption: $150m affected currency exposure × 8 bps annual risk-buffer cost reduction = $120k/year after hedge fees. Benefit over 24 months = B×(24−lag)/12=$160.0k; ROI=(benefit−C)/C=-11.1%. Half/base/1.5× benefit sensitivity -55.6%/-11.1%/+33.3%; zero benefit −100%; break-even annual benefit $135.0k. No actual adoption, market capacity, realized P&L or cash savings established; validate opportunity overlap and costs before business case.
**Publication potential:** Joint FX collateral tail-risk hedging. **Novelty:** Unlike cross-currency repo routing or static haircut clustering: hedge currency mismatch on an already selected loan/pledge. **Tools proposed:** cvxpy,numpy,scipy.
