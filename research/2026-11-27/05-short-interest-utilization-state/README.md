## 05 — Short Interest versus Securities Loan Utilization State Decomposition
**A — Securities finance | US exchange-listed equities | 92/100 | Medium-high | Conditional on data, capacity and validation; synthetic prototype feasible**
**Problem and decision:** Determine when borrow utilization reflects loan-market supply contraction versus directional short demand. Existing comparator (autoregression on utilization and market returns) may fail under short interest is not total loans, coverage differs by vendor and share class.
**H1 (proposed):** Release-aligned short-interest anchors reduce 10-day out-of-sample utilization forecast MAE by 10% versus an autoregression.
**Mathematics:** U_t=L_t/S_t; Δlog U=Δlog L−Δlog S; latent L demand and S lendable inventory in positive state-space; SI published observation is noisy proxy, not identity.
**Data/grain:** issuer × share class × trading day / short-interest publication; reported SI, lender availability/utilization, corporate actions, lendable supply and fees. **Clock:** ingest FINRA publication after 4:40 p.m. ET or later actual receipt; trade next eligible session.
**Baseline → model → advanced:** autoregression on utilization and market returns → constrained state-space model with delayed SI observation → hierarchical particle filter with split-adjusted latent demand/supply regime.
**Evaluation:** MAE, calibration, supply/demand label precision on verified events. **Go/no-go:** ≥10% MAE reduction, bootstrap lower bound positive; abstain when components unidentifiable. **Negative tests:** synthetic split, delayed publication, denominator near zero, shuffled release labels.
**Sources:** [FINRA API documentation](https://developer.finra.org/docs); [ESMA EU SFT market overview (9 April 2024)](https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esma-publishes-first-overview-eu-securities-financing-transactions-markets). Licensed: licensed historical lendable supply and loan balances. Permissioned: approved anonymized lending-event observations for state labels.
**Illustrative 24-month scenario (USD thousands; not empirical ROI):** assumed total build/data/maintenance cost C=$160k; annual steady-state net operating benefit before project cost B=$100k; launch lag 7 months, no benefit during lag. Capacity assumption: $200m addressable repricing inventory × 5 bps net annual improvement = $100k/year; conditional on actual policy benefit. Benefit over 24 months = B×(24−lag)/12=$141.7k; ROI=(benefit−C)/C=-11.5%. Half/base/1.5× benefit sensitivity -55.7%/-11.5%/+32.8%; zero benefit −100%; break-even annual benefit $112.9k. No actual adoption, market capacity, realized P&L or cash savings established; validate opportunity overlap and costs before business case.
**Publication potential:** Mixed-frequency latent-state identification in lending markets. **Novelty:** Not fee selection-bias correction or borrow→market-depth causality: infer supply versus demand states with delayed public anchors. **Tools proposed:** statsmodels,pymc,numpy.
