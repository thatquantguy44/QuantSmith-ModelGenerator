## 15 — Accrual Cash-Conversion Reversal with XBRL Restatement Lineage
**B — Domestic/global markets | US and foreign SEC reporting equities | 91/100 | Medium | High for deterministic/public-data prototype; market claims blocked**
**Problem and decision:** Distinguish operating cash conversion weakness from reporting reclassification before analyst forecast updates. Existing comparator (total accrual ratio plus sector and size) may fail under taxonomy changes, YTD versus quarter flows, acquisition effects, lookahead from amendments.
**H1 (proposed):** Accession-aware working-capital components reduce next-year cash-flow forecast MAE by 10% versus total accrual ratio.
**Mathematics:** A=(NI−CFO)/avg_assets; y=CFO_t+4/assets; y=α+β A+γ ΔAR/assets+δ Δinventory/assets+controls. Units currency matched; signs consistent; no amended values retroactive.
**Data/grain:** issuer CIK × fiscal quarter × filing accession × tag; cash flow, net income, assets, receivables, inventory, restatements, taxonomy mappings and delisting records. **Clock:** first filing acceptance/dissemination, with later amended accession separate; decisions next session.
**Baseline → model → advanced:** total accrual ratio plus sector and size → sparse panel with accession-aware working-capital features → hierarchical accounting graph with semantic tag mapping and abstention.
**Evaluation:** cash-flow MAE, tag mapping precision, forecast IC, coverage. **Go/no-go:** ≥10% MAE improvement; ≥98% mapping precision on hand labels; abstain on ambiguity. **Negative tests:** YTD-to-quarter conversion, duplicate tag contexts, unit mismatch, 10-K/A future amendment.
**Sources:** [SEC EDGAR API documentation (6 June 2024)](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces); [SEC financial statement and notes data](https://www.sec.gov/data-research/sec-markets-data/financial-statement-notes-data-sets). Licensed: licensed split-adjusted/delisted returns, estimates and corporate actions. Permissioned: approved research analyst labels only.
**Illustrative 24-month scenario (USD thousands; not empirical ROI):** assumed total build/data/maintenance cost C=$115k; annual steady-state net operating benefit before project cost B=$108k; launch lag 4 months, no benefit during lag. Capacity assumption: $90m research sleeve × 12 bps annual net benefit=$108k/year; no measured factor return. Benefit over 24 months = B×(24−lag)/12=$180.0k; ROI=(benefit−C)/C=+56.5%. Half/base/1.5× benefit sensitivity -21.7%/+56.5%/+134.8%; zero benefit −100%; break-even annual benefit $69.0k. No actual adoption, market capacity, realized P&L or cash savings established; validate opportunity overlap and costs before business case.
**Publication potential:** Financial statement measurement and reproducible accrual research. **Novelty:** Different from macro vintage engine: accounting lineage and operating cash conversion, not macro release abstention. **Tools proposed:** polars,lxml,statsmodels.
