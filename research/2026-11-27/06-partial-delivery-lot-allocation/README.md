## 06 — Partial Securities Delivery Allocation under Minimum Lot Rules
**A — Securities finance | US and European securities settlement | 91/100 | Medium | Conditional on data, capacity and validation; synthetic prototype feasible**
**Problem and decision:** Allocate limited deliverable shares across obligations when partial settlement is permitted. Existing comparator (earliest-deadline-first full delivery) may fail under partial processing overhead and legal restrictions differ by venue.
**H1 (proposed):** Quantity-aware integer allocation reduces weighted unmet shares by 10% versus earliest-deadline full delivery.
**Mathematics:** min Σ_j w_j(Q_j−x_j)+Σ_j f_j 1{x_j>0}; 0≤x_j≤Q_j, Σ x_j≤I, x_j multiples of m_j; permission and cutoffs hard. Q required, x delivered, I available, w shortfall cost, f fixed processing cost.
**Data/grain:** security × settlement instruction × cutoff snapshot; remaining obligations, settled inventory, minimum lot, partial permission, costs, deadlines and acknowledgments. **Clock:** before each cutoff with confirmed deliverable stock; pending receipts excluded.
**Baseline → model → advanced:** earliest-deadline-first full delivery → integer allocation with minimum partial sizes → two-stage stochastic delivery with confirmed receipts and recourse.
**Evaluation:** weighted undelivered shares, net fail fees, message count, missed cutoffs. **Go/no-go:** ≥10% lower weighted unmet shares and non-increasing net costs; zero impermissible partials. **Negative tests:** lot above remainder; negative inventory; all-or-nothing flag; delayed acknowledgement.
**Sources:** [DTCC rules and procedures](https://www.dtcc.com/legal/rules-and-procedures); [ESMA EU SFT market overview (9 April 2024)](https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esma-publishes-first-overview-eu-securities-financing-transactions-markets). Licensed: settlement security master and rule/fee data. Permissioned: approved obligation snapshots and partial-settlement terms.
**Illustrative 24-month scenario (USD thousands; not empirical ROI):** assumed total build/data/maintenance cost C=$125k; annual steady-state net operating benefit before project cost B=$160k; launch lag 5 months, no benefit during lag. Capacity assumption: 40k eligible instructions/year × $4 avoidable weighted cost = $160k/year, after message costs. Benefit over 24 months = B×(24−lag)/12=$253.3k; ROI=(benefit−C)/C=+102.7%. Half/base/1.5× benefit sensitivity +1.3%/+102.7%/+204.0%; zero benefit −100%; break-even annual benefit $78.9k. No actual adoption, market capacity, realized P&L or cash savings established; validate opportunity overlap and costs before business case.
**Publication potential:** Discrete resource allocation with settlement microstructure. **Novelty:** Prior CSDR queue ranking handles order; this selects permitted quantities and indivisible partials, not contagion or buy-in timing. **Tools proposed:** ortools,simpy,polars.
