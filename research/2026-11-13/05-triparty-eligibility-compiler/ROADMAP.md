# ROADMAP — Tri-Party Collateral Eligibility Clause-to-Constraint Compiler
## Stage 0 — evidence feasibility (weeks 1–2)
- Validate public data meaning, historical vintages, publication lag and any commercial license for **US triparty and secured funding**.
- Confirm action/market legal constraints, unit conventions and contract clause × effective version × collateral asset × valuation time.
- Gate: stop if input authorization, comparable baseline or event-time record is unavailable.

## Stage 1 — reproducible baseline (weeks 3–4)
- Create synthetic event fixtures with adverse missing/revision scenarios.
- Implement Rule tables hand-transcribed and checked by two human operators. and hard feasibility tests.
- Gate: AC-001, AC-002, AC-005.

## Stage 2 — candidate science (weeks 5–8)
- Implement Schema-constrained text extraction with symbolic eligibility evaluation and abstention., compare matched chronological holdouts, audit Natural-language ambiguity, contract precedence and jurisdiction-specific clauses make full automation unsafe..
- Consider Contradiction-aware graph of rule amendments with formal test generation and reviewer feedback. only on preregistered incremental value.
- Gate: Reject if any critical false acceptance appears in stress fixtures or human review burden fails to improve.

## Stage 3 — controlled shadow evaluation (weeks 9–12)
- Measure false eligibility acceptance rate, false rejection rate, review labor, coverage, abstention, parse latency., the relevant capacity constraints and all-in implementation cost.
- Replace illustrative 24-month ROI (-57% to 66%) with source-backed measured economics or explicitly retain uncertainty.
- Gate: independent risk/compliance review, adequate tail robustness, reproducibility evidence.

## Deferred production
Requires separate feature PR, CI, audit controls, monitoring, rollback and human authorization. No live trading or private data is authorized by this package.
