## ROI definition and sensitivity
Every project has a **hypothetical** two-year total cost C and hypothetical total gross economic benefit range B_low–B_high. Net ROI=(B−C)/C. These are transparent *screening scenarios*, not forecasts, validated returns, cash flows, Sharpe ratios or proprietary estimates. Many low cases are negative. Actual capacity, funding, market access, taxes, capital and labor costs require measurement before approval.

## Research evidence context, as checked November 13, 2026
- [SEC Treasury clearing](https://www.sec.gov/featured-topics/treasury-clearing-implementation) lists deadlines for eligible cash (Dec. 31, 2026) and repo (June 30, 2027); actual clearing eligibility/exemptions require legal review.
- [OFR repo release](https://www.financialresearch.gov/short-term-funding-monitor/datasets/repo/) publicly describes one-business-day and two-business-day lags for centrally cleared and tri-party repo respectively, and confidentiality-related missing values.
- [CFTC COT](https://www.cftc.gov/MarketReports/CommitmentsofTraders/index.htm) represents Tuesday positions that become public later; use publication timestamps.
- [ALFRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/docs/api/fred/realtime_period.html) supports historical macro vintages; series-specific quality checks remain necessary.
- [UN Comtrade API](https://comtradeplus.un.org/en/api-docs) has no-key previews but full extracts can require keys or premium products.
