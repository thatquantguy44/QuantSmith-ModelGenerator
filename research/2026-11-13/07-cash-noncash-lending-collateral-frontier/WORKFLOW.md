## Ordered workflow
**1. Ask a testable question:** An all-in haircut, rebate and risk-adjusted price frontier improves loan profitability versus fee-only collateral preference.
**2. Set the decision:** Price and select borrower-offered collateral methods within owner mandate and agent legal permission.
**3. Acquire data:** Cash and noncash terms, rebate schedules, reinvestment constraints, asset eligibility, mark-to-market, haircuts and legal restrictions.
**4. Freeze the temporal boundary:** quote acceptance before loan opening with known collateral terms, not end-of-day realized earnings
**5. Run baseline:** Choose the highest headline loan spread among eligible offers.
**6. Evaluate candidate:** Loan-specific all-in economic break-even and constraints solver.
**7. Challenge method:** Robust multi-asset collateral frontier with convex liquidity tail-risk charge., only if justified.
**8. Measure:** net economic revenue, risk capital per dollar, haircuts and eligibility breaches, retained lending volume.
**9. Stress:** Comparing unavailable cash reinvestment yield to executable noncash economics creates phantom spread.
**10. Fail/continue rule:** Reject if no incremental cost-adjusted profit after retaining legally admissible and service-quality constraints.

For every failed gate record what was unavailable and why. All described execution stages are research plans, not completed model runs.
