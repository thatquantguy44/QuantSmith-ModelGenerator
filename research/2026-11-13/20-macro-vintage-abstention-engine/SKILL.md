1. Load SPEC and HANDOFF; state hypothesis and category B · Domestic & global markets.
2. Build source-claim ledger. Do not conflate official data description and available executable market dataset.
3. Check first public availability and carry correction vintages forward, never backward.
4. Create adverse synthetic fixtures and reject invalid market constraints.
5. Fit and run **Always-on macro momentum score built from latest-vintage series.** on training data with full costs.
6. Define **As-of macro feature store with release-confidence model and abstention.**, plus optional **Bayesian missing-data multiple-vintage filtering and conformal selective prediction with capacity-aware portfolio overlay.** conditional on simple model advantage.
7. Freeze chronological holdouts and evaluate **revision-induced backtest bias, selective risk loss, coverage, false alarm rate, time-to-data-availability.** plus capacity and tail risk.
8. Try to falsify: **Reject if abstention fails to reduce loss on corrected vintage history or simply abstains from all market decisions.**.
9. Record all negative tests, unverified sources, measured trade-offs and manual approval gate.
10. Return BLOCKED if ALFRED historical series coverage, future revisions and OECD methodological breaks can be incomplete; abstention selection bias. cannot be mitigated.
