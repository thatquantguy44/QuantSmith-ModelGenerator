# Reusable cross-idea AI research workflow — Batch 17
1. **Discovery agent** reads the archive and compares candidate economic decisions to existing hypotheses; outputs overlap flags, not generic synonyms.
2. **Evidence agent** verifies original primary source URL, publication date, licence and available_at; explicitly distinguishes portal vs granular dataset.
3. **Quant analyst** writes coherent math with defined variables, domain units and operational constraints; estimates an appropriate no-action and practical baseline.
4. **Synthetic fixture agent** creates proposed data/edge-case schema; **must never ingest confidential records** in public GitHub.
5. **Research experiment agent** specifies candidate and advanced methods, train/test windows, embargo and falsification with negative controls.
6. **Independent verification agent** reviews financial claims, no-lookahead, executable capacity and cost assumptions; failure is recorded rather than rationalized.
7. **Publication agent** creates one date-scoped research PR and Slack digest, never auto-merges; a separate human approves economic/deployment gate.

Shared future modules: `qmg_core.sources`, `qmg_core.temporal`, `qmg_core.contracts`, `qmg_core.economics`, `qmg_core.optimization`, `qmg_core.validation`, `qmg_core.evidence`, and 20 `qmg.research.*` namespaces. **These do not exist in this documentation-only PR.**
