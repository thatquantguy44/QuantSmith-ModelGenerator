Information gate: Valuation at contract initiation using curve observed by quote cut-off, not future realized policy rates.
Potential release revisions: Floor rights and term structures vary by legal agreement; historic negative-rate regimes have sample limitations.
A published source portal may carry a dataset whose latest revision would bias a historical test. Maintain immutable release and retrieval ledger with a quote/transaction observation clock.
