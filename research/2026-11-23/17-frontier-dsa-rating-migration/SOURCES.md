## Source-specific controls
Required panel: Country IMF/World Bank public DSA documents, sovereign events, IMF debt statistics, bond quotes and GDP vintages
Information gate: Country report posting time and rating applicability; embargo unpublished staff papers and revisions.
Potential release revisions: DSA coverage selection and program endogeneity, non-tradable distressed bonds, rare default events.
A published source portal may carry a dataset whose latest revision would bias a historical test. Maintain immutable release and retrieval ledger with a quote/transaction observation clock.
