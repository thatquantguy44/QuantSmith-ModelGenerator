# SKILL — Sovereign DSA Rating Migration Event Pricing for Frontier Bonds
## When to use
When evaluating: Prioritize bond credit watchlists around IMF/World Bank debt sustainability status changes.

## Preconditions
Source entitlement, as-of clock and accounting convention for Country IMF/World Bank public DSA documents, sovereign events, IMF debt statistics, bond quotes and GDP vintages; a defined decision and costed baseline.

## Procedure
1. Freeze H1: First-public DSA risk-rating changes improve post-publication sovereign credit-loss calibration versus debt/GDP screens.; define H0 with cost-inclusive `Latest public debt-to-GDP and reserves ratio`.
2. Verify official pages and specific historical source versions. Country report posting time and rating applicability; embargo unpublished staff papers and revisions.
3. Build deterministic synthetic fixtures including: DSA coverage selection and program endogeneity, non-tradable distressed bonds, rare default events..
4. Implement the baseline before trying `Multi-state ordinal risk migration model with publication-aligned survival validation`.
5. Test `Bayesian sovereign hazard with IMF policy intervention and restructuring recovery scenarios` only if the simpler candidate survives.
6. Reconstruct economic costs and capacity: Frontier bond sizes/spreads can defeat excess returns; focus risk-control research first..
7. Report Default Brier score, out-of-time risk precision, spread change, no-trade capacity flags plus negative-control confidence bounds.
8. Stop if: No improvement over conservative sovereign ratings baseline, or price move entirely before public timestamp..
9. Prepare code hash, evidence ledger, and independent human review. No trading.
