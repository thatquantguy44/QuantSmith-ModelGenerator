# SKILL — Agency MBS Dollar-Roll Cheapest-to-Deliver Financing Option
## When to use
When evaluating: Select TBA dollar-roll financing and permissible pool delivery sets versus holding specified pools financed through repo.

## Preconditions
Source entitlement, as-of clock and accounting convention for TBA front/back prices, pools, payups, coupon schedules, specified-pool risk, MBSD netting, EPN eligibility and execution costs; a defined decision and costed baseline.

## Procedure
1. Freeze H1: Explicit deliverability and pool opportunity costs explain dollar-roll implied funding differences beyond coupon, term and simple financing spreads.; define H0 with cost-inclusive `Dollar-roll implied financing vs same-tenor generic repo without delivery-option valuation`.
2. Verify official pages and specific historical source versions. Decision at pre-roll executable quote; EPN pool assignment becomes known later and must not leak into earlier signals.
3. Build deterministic synthetic fixtures including: Pool price selection, payup optionality, actual pool availability and post-trade EPN leakage..
4. Implement the baseline before trying `Constrained pool-delivery optimizer with observable pool financing carry and roll prices`.
5. Test `Stochastic pool quality and delivery-selection real-options game with cheapest-deliverable simulation` only if the simpler candidate survives.
6. Reconstruct economic costs and capacity: Market depth and eligible pool inventory limit size; include bid/ask, delivery allocations, financing and clearing margin..
7. Report Net financing bps, cheapest-deliverable premium, pre-settlement fail exposure, netted capital and tail regret plus negative-control confidence bounds.
8. Stop if: No stable out-of-sample cost-adjusted advantage over delivery-aware manual baseline at realistic pool eligibility..
9. Prepare code hash, evidence ledger, and independent human review. No trading.
