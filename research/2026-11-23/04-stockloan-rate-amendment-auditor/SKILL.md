# SKILL — Securities Loan Retroactive Rate Amendment Revenue Assurance
## When to use
When evaluating: Identify contractually unsupported retroactive fee amendments before invoice posting.

## Preconditions
Source entitlement, as-of clock and accounting convention for Synthetic loans, amendment lifecycle, dated fee notices, loan terminations, currency and day-count conventions; a defined decision and costed baseline.

## Procedure
1. Freeze H1: Bitemporal contract-and-loan event reconstruction detects more true billing exceptions than end-of-day price reconciliation without increasing false disputes.; define H0 with cost-inclusive `Latest agreed fee × end-period balance × elapsed days`.
2. Verify official pages and specific historical source versions. Invoice run includes only fee amendments authorized and received before invoice cut-off, even if effective date is earlier.
3. Build deterministic synthetic fixtures including: Contract-level bilateral waiver rules, fee confirmation ambiguity and out-of-order event feeds..
4. Implement the baseline before trying `Bitemporal finite-state rate and loan lifecycle ledger with invariant checks`.
5. Test `Probabilistic anomaly prioritization atop an exact contract-aware billing engine` only if the simpler candidate survives.
6. Reconstruct economic costs and capacity: Value in avoided losses and less manual rework, not trading alpha; false dispute costs and review minutes cap benefit..
7. Report Synthetic exception precision/recall, manual hours, invoice basis-point leakage, false escalation rate plus negative-control confidence bounds.
8. Stop if: No improvement in high-severity seeded exceptions relative to deterministic ledger, or unauthorized dispute actions..
9. Prepare code hash, evidence ledger, and independent human review. No trading.
