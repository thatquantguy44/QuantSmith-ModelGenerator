## Source-specific controls
Required panel: Synthetic loans, amendment lifecycle, dated fee notices, loan terminations, currency and day-count conventions
Information gate: Invoice run includes only fee amendments authorized and received before invoice cut-off, even if effective date is earlier.
Potential release revisions: Contract-level bilateral waiver rules, fee confirmation ambiguity and out-of-order event feeds.
A published source portal may carry a dataset whose latest revision would bias a historical test. Maintain immutable release and retrieval ledger with a quote/transaction observation clock.
