## Procedure
1. Freeze H1: Exogenous changes to borrow supply lower subsequent displayed depth/increase effective spreads more than matched control stocks.; define H0 with cost-inclusive `Spread/depth forecasting using only realized volatility and market cap`.
2. Verify official pages and specific historical source versions. Quote-decision timing after actual supply notice; event timestamps distinguish pre-event leak, regular session and close.
3. Build deterministic synthetic fixtures including: Selection on distressed shorts, simultaneous volatility and hard-to-borrow endogenous events..
4. Implement the baseline before trying `Matched differences-in-differences/event-time instrumental analysis`.
5. Test `Structural market-maker inventory equilibrium with dynamic borrowing constraint` only if the simpler candidate survives.
6. Reconstruct economic costs and capacity: Not an unconditional trading signal; capacity depends on quote depth and borrow clearance, include adverse selection..
7. Report Event-time β confidence interval, placebos, effective spread, depth and incremental risk price plus negative-control confidence bounds.
8. Stop if: No pre-registered effect with valid pretrends/instruments or effect only on illiquid untradeable names..
9. Prepare code hash, evidence ledger, and independent human review. No trading.
