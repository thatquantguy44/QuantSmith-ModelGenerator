# SOURCES — Corporate Bond Securities Lending from CDS Basis Hedge Demand
**Evidence state (Nov 23, 2026): Verified official public source *pages*; exact required series, licence, API accessibility and historical publication clock remain unresolved.** Context links are not proof of alpha, data availability, or paper novelty.

## Tier 1 — publicly accessible starting points
- **SEC EDGAR Data Access** — Issuer disclosure with explicit EDGAR acceptance/dissemination clock. [Official source](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-search-assistance/accessing-edgar-data). Verified public page as of 2026-11-23; not verification of exact dataset access or licence.
- **New York Fed Primary Dealer Statistics** — Weekly aggregated financing/positions/fails, delayed. [Official source](https://www.newyorkfed.org/markets/primarydealers.html). Verified public page as of 2026-11-23; not verification of exact dataset access or licence.

## Tier 2 — commercial/licensed
Corporate bond TRACE histories, CDS quotes and bond lending locates. Obtain lawful entitlement, historical versions, executable bid/ask and permitted redistribution terms first. Never extract Cboe delayed quote tables programmatically where prohibited.

## Tier 3 — strictly permissioned / not in public GitHub
Permissioned borrower requests/loan data. No account positions, loan fees, counterparties, contracts or employer systems are included.

## Source-specific controls
Required panel: CUSIP-level corporate bond lending fees/locates, CDS curves, TRACE trade activity, issue amount, corporate actions
Information gate: CDS and cash-bond spreads only after published executable quote times; lending fills observed subsequently.
Potential release revisions: Non-executable CDS quotes, bond TRACE delay and endogeneity of credit distress.
A published source portal may carry a dataset whose latest revision would bias a historical test. Maintain immutable release and retrieval ledger with a quote/transaction observation clock.
