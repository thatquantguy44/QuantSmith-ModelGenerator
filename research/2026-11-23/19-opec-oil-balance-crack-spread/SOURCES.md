## Source-specific controls
Required panel: JODI monthly crude stocks/flows, OPEC reports, EIA refinery/product balances, crack-futures settlements
Information gate: JODI typically publishes next-month and revises; align release date, not reporting month; execute only after release.
Potential release revisions: Monthly country submission corrections, unit conversion mismatches and sanctioned flow missingness.
A published source portal may carry a dataset whose latest revision would bias a historical test. Maintain immutable release and retrieval ledger with a quote/transaction observation clock.
