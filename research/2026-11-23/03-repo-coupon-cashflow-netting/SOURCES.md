## Tier 3 — strictly permissioned / not in public GitHub
Authorized repo contracts and cash-settlement messages. No account positions, loan fees, counterparties, contracts or employer systems are included.

## Source-specific controls
Required panel: Public bond schedules, ex-coupon dates, settlement calendars, synthetic counterparties and netting agreements
Information gate: Coupon schedule published at issuance; adjustments and confirmed obligations only after contract notice timestamp.
Potential release revisions: Wrong beneficial-ownership treatment, netting rights and special cash settlement terms.
A published source portal may carry a dataset whose latest revision would bias a historical test. Maintain immutable release and retrieval ledger with a quote/transaction observation clock.
