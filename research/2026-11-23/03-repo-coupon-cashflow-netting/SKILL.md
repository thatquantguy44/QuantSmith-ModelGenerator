# SKILL — Repo Coupon and Principal Event Cash-Flow Netting Scheduler
## When to use
When evaluating: Minimize same-day cash deficits caused by collateral coupon/principal flows and contractual manufactured payments.

## Preconditions
Source entitlement, as-of clock and accounting convention for Public bond schedules, ex-coupon dates, settlement calendars, synthetic counterparties and netting agreements; a defined decision and costed baseline.

## Procedure
1. Freeze H1: Event-aware legally permissible netting reduces liquidity buffer and operational exceptions versus cash-flow-independent settlement.; define H0 with cost-inclusive `Gross independent payment processing with static buffer`.
2. Verify official pages and specific historical source versions. Coupon schedule published at issuance; adjustments and confirmed obligations only after contract notice timestamp.
3. Build deterministic synthetic fixtures including: Wrong beneficial-ownership treatment, netting rights and special cash settlement terms..
4. Implement the baseline before trying `Mixed-integer liquidity scheduling with eligibility/netting-set constraints`.
5. Test `Stochastic event-flow MPC with missed payment penalties and legal-entity netting restrictions` only if the simpler candidate survives.
6. Reconstruct economic costs and capacity: Savings limited to payments legally eligible for netting; reconciliation costs, daylight overdraft fees and solver latency included..
7. Report Peak cash buffer, gross payment count, exception cost, settlement timeliness plus negative-control confidence bounds.
8. Stop if: No capacity-adjusted cost reduction versus pre-netted conventional operations or violations of payment rights..
9. Prepare code hash, evidence ledger, and independent human review. No trading.
