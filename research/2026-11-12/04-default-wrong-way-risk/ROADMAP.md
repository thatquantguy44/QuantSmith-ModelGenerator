# ROADMAP — Borrower Default Wrong-Way Risk Optimization
**Week 1–2: Feasibility.** Validate source access for Counterparty credit, collateral, contract closeout provisions, stress price and liquidity history; confirm price borrower limits, haircut and contingent indemnification, legal restrictions, unit, economics and initial research cost. Stop if licensed data, event clock or legally possible alternatives are absent.
**Week 3–4: Baseline.** Establish synthetic fixtures and a cost-inclusive `independent PD times static haircut` simulator. Gate: AC-001/AC-002 and signed specification.
**Week 5–8: Candidate.** Prototype `correlated default/exposure Monte Carlo`; consider `stochastic-intensity wrong-way closeout with price impact` only after a controlled ablation. Gate: interpretable holdout comparison and no future information.
**Week 9–12: Shadow review.** Present `stress ES, loss calibration, limits used and risk-adjusted P&L`, confidence ranges, incident/risk scenarios and break-even costs. Stop if advantage does not survive costs or sparse default observations and unidentifiable tail parameters.
**Beyond:** Human approval before any real data connection or live integration. No deployment is implied.
