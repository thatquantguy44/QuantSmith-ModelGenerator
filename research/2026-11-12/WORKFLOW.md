# Batch publication workflow
1. Validate 15 unique hypotheses and proposed economic decisions.
2. Confirm source links and identify actual data-access licensing separately.
3. Write idea-specific SPEC→PLAN→TASKS→EVALS and agent contracts.
4. Generate synthetically testable baselines and falsification requirements; no fabricated empirical results.
5. Count and inspect all committed documents. Verify PR file list and summarize limitations.
6. Post one Slack digest with verified PR link; human reviewer merges only after approval.

**Control:** Legal eligibility, entitlement and information barriers override prospective revenue optimizations.
