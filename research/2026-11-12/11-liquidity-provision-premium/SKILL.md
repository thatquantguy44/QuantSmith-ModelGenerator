# Reusable skill — Securities Lending Liquidity Provision Premium
## Trigger
When deciding how to **value dependable securities supply during high financing stress**.
## Preconditions
Authoritative SPEC/PLAN, confirmed timestamp and unit, authorized access to Lender inventory changes, special fees, recalls, utilization, systemic stress factors, approved risk and cost model.
## Method
1. Restate hypothesis and observable decision.
2. Build immutable source and licensing ledger; reject inaccessible private data.
3. Create point-in-time synthetic fixture for liquidity-provision-premium; forbid future updates.
4. Rebuild trailing revenue per lendable dollar with real costs and legal constraints.
5. Specify conditional financing factor decomposition, then optionally endogenous supply/fee equilibrium with nonlinear downside beta.
6. Run time-split/paired holdout evaluation: stress carry, stability, adjusted yield and mandate value.
7. Stress primary threat: composition and survivorship bias can masquerade as a premium.
8. Write falsification, negative findings and exact run manifest.
9. Update HANDOFF truthfully; request human risk and economic review.
## Output
Proposed `run_manifest.json`, `claims.md`, `metrics.json`, `results.md`; none exist yet.
## Reuse
Share time-alignment, economics, source-rights checks and synthetic fixtures with other QMG skills, but keep the domain-specific model scoped here.
