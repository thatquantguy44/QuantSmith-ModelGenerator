# SOURCES — Financing Execution Shortfall Model
## Open/public starting points (official verified portals)
- [New York Fed SOFR data](https://www.newyorkfed.org/markets/reference-rates/sofr) — verified public institutional landing page, NOT proof of an available granular series.
- [BIS research and statistics](https://www.bis.org/) — verified public institutional landing page, NOT proof of an available granular series.
These are **context/source entry points**, not confirmation that Timestamped RFQs, quotes, fill/rejects, booked fees, market moves and settlement is available for download at required precision.
## Licensed / commercial
- licensed executable reference quotes. Inspect license, as-of coverage, revisions, permissible uses, price, and redistribution restrictions.
## Permissioned internal, not to be committed
- authorized RFQ and transaction lifecycle logs. May only be accessed in an authorized private environment, with anonymization and entitlement controls.
## Source diligence
Build a data dictionary for Timestamped RFQs, quotes, fill/rejects, booked fees, market moves and settlement, with effective time vs published time, market clock, methodology, survivorship and audit citation. Add verified DOI links for relevant academic papers when discovered; none have been certified in this initial batch.
