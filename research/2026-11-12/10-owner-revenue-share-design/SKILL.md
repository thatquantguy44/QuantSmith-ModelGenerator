# Reusable skill — Beneficial-Owner Revenue-Sharing Mechanism
## Trigger
When deciding how to **design fair participation-aware lending revenue splits**.
## Preconditions
Authoritative SPEC/PLAN, confirmed timestamp and unit, authorized access to Owner fee splits, available holdings, program restrictions, lending volume and recalls, approved risk and cost model.
## Method
1. Restate hypothesis and observable decision.
2. Build immutable source and licensing ledger; reject inaccessible private data.
3. Create point-in-time synthetic fixture for owner-revenue-share-design; forbid future updates.
4. Rebuild fixed schedule of revenue sharing with real costs and legal constraints.
5. Specify principal-agent participation and supply elasticity, then optionally hierarchical Bayesian mechanism design with owner heterogeneity.
6. Run time-split/paired holdout evaluation: new supply, agent revenue, owner revenue, program retention.
7. Stress primary threat: unobservable selection and fairness/fiduciary restrictions.
8. Write falsification, negative findings and exact run manifest.
9. Update HANDOFF truthfully; request human risk and economic review.
## Output
Proposed `run_manifest.json`, `claims.md`, `metrics.json`, `results.md`; none exist yet.
## Reuse
Share time-alignment, economics, source-rights checks and synthetic fixtures with other QMG skills, but keep the domain-specific model scoped here.
