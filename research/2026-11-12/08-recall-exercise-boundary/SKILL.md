# Reusable skill — Loan Recall Early-Exercise Boundary
## Trigger
When deciding how to **forecast compliant beneficial-owner recall exercise and source replacement**.
## Preconditions
Authoritative SPEC/PLAN, confirmed timestamp and unit, authorized access to Loan age, fee, recalls, corporate action calendar, owner restrictions, replacement cost, approved risk and cost model.
## Method
1. Restate hypothesis and observable decision.
2. Build immutable source and licensing ledger; reject inaccessible private data.
3. Create point-in-time synthetic fixture for recall-exercise-boundary; forbid future updates.
4. Rebuild constant owner-specific recall probability with real costs and legal constraints.
5. Specify constrained survival and stopping model, then optionally least-squares Monte Carlo owner exercise policy.
6. Run time-split/paired holdout evaluation: recall warning, replacement cost, calibration and fail prevention.
7. Stress primary threat: governance and fiduciary rights cannot be traded away.
8. Write falsification, negative findings and exact run manifest.
9. Update HANDOFF truthfully; request human risk and economic review.
## Output
Proposed `run_manifest.json`, `claims.md`, `metrics.json`, `results.md`; none exist yet.
## Reuse
Share time-alignment, economics, source-rights checks and synthetic fixtures with other QMG skills, but keep the domain-specific model scoped here.
