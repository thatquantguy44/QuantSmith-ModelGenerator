# SOURCES — Loan Recall Early-Exercise Boundary
## Open/public starting points (official verified portals)
- [BIS research and statistics](https://www.bis.org/) — verified public institutional landing page, NOT proof of an available granular series.
- [FRED economic data](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/) — verified public institutional landing page, NOT proof of an available granular series.
These are **context/source entry points**, not confirmation that Loan age, fee, recalls, corporate action calendar, owner restrictions, replacement cost is available for download at required precision.
## Licensed / commercial
- licensed proxy calendar, ownership and corporate-event data. Inspect license, as-of coverage, revisions, permissible uses, price, and redistribution restrictions.
## Permissioned internal, not to be committed
- owner-approved recall and loan history. May only be accessed in an authorized private environment, with anonymization and entitlement controls.

Build a data dictionary for Loan age, fee, recalls, corporate action calendar, owner restrictions, replacement cost, with effective time vs published time, market clock, methodology, survivorship and audit citation.
