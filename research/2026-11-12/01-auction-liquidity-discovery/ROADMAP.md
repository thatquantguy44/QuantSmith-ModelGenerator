# ROADMAP — Auction-Based Securities Lending Liquidity Discovery
**Week 1–2: Feasibility.** Validate source access for Timestamped locate quotes, revisions, rejects, fills, quantities and source-specific inventory; confirm set fee and allocation for scarce lendable securities, legal restrictions, unit, economics and initial research cost. Stop if licensed data, event clock or legally possible alternatives are absent.
**Week 3–4: Baseline.** Establish synthetic fixtures and a cost-inclusive `highest-fee-first feasible allocation` simulator. Gate: AC-001/AC-002 and signed specification.
**Week 5–8: Candidate.** Prototype `censored structural demand + constrained auction`; consider `hierarchical Bayesian mechanism design` only after a controlled ablation. Gate: interpretable holdout comparison and no future information.
**Week 9–12: Shadow review.** Present `net revenue, allocative efficiency, fill rate, churn and regret`, confidence ranges, incident/risk scenarios and break-even costs. Stop if advantage does not survive costs or censored demand, endogenous quote selection.
**Beyond:** Human approval before any real data connection or live integration. No deployment is implied.
