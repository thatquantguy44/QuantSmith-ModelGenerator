# Reusable skill — Auction-Based Securities Lending Liquidity Discovery
## Trigger
When deciding how to **set fee and allocation for scarce lendable securities**.
## Preconditions
Authoritative SPEC/PLAN, confirmed timestamp and unit, authorized access to Timestamped locate quotes, revisions, rejects, fills, quantities and source-specific inventory, approved risk and cost model.
## Method
1. Restate hypothesis and observable decision.
2. Build immutable source and licensing ledger; reject inaccessible private data.
3. Create point-in-time synthetic fixture for auction-liquidity-discovery; forbid future updates.
4. Rebuild highest-fee-first feasible allocation with real costs and legal constraints.
5. Specify censored structural demand + constrained auction, then optionally hierarchical Bayesian mechanism design.
6. Run time-split/paired holdout evaluation: net revenue, allocative efficiency, fill rate, churn and regret.
7. Stress primary threat: censored demand, endogenous quote selection.
8. Write falsification, negative findings and exact run manifest.
9. Update HANDOFF truthfully; request human risk and economic review.
## Output
Proposed `run_manifest.json`, `claims.md`, `metrics.json`, `results.md`; none exist yet.
## Reuse
Share time-alignment, economics, source-rights checks and synthetic fixtures with other QMG skills, but keep the domain-specific model scoped here.
