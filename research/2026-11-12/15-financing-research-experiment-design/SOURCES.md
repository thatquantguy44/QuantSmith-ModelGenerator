# SOURCES — Optimal Financing Research Experiment Design
## Open/public starting points (official verified portals)
- [FRED economic data](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/) — verified public institutional landing page, NOT proof of an available granular series.
- [BIS research and statistics](https://www.bis.org/) — verified public institutional landing page, NOT proof of an available granular series.
These are **context/source entry points**, not confirmation that Experiment budgets, model alternatives, synthetic case studies, past evidence quality and reproducibility logs is available for download at required precision.
## Licensed / commercial
- licensed data only for experiments that require it. Inspect license, as-of coverage, revisions, permissible uses, price, and redistribution restrictions.
## Permissioned internal, not to be committed
- sanitized project outcomes; no internal research corpus in public repo. May only be accessed in an authorized private environment, with anonymization and entitlement controls.
## Source diligence
