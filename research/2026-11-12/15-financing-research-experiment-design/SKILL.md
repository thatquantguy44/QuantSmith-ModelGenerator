# Reusable skill — Optimal Financing Research Experiment Design
## Trigger
When deciding how to **allocate scarce research effort to high information-value experiments**.
## Preconditions
Authoritative SPEC/PLAN, confirmed timestamp and unit, authorized access to Experiment budgets, model alternatives, synthetic case studies, past evidence quality and reproducibility logs, approved risk and cost model.
## Method
1. Restate hypothesis and observable decision.
2. Build immutable source and licensing ledger; reject inaccessible private data.
3. Create point-in-time synthetic fixture for financing-research-experiment-design; forbid future updates.
4. Rebuild equal effort per idea with real costs and legal constraints.
5. Specify Bayesian value-of-information and sequential experiment selection, then optionally knowledge-gradient portfolio policy with correlated research tasks.
6. Run time-split/paired holdout evaluation: decision value per research hour, false discovery, stopping time and deployed value.
7. Stress primary threat: retrospective outcomes are selectively recorded and success probabilities subjective.
8. Write falsification, negative findings and exact run manifest.
9. Update HANDOFF truthfully; request human risk and economic review.
## Output
Proposed `run_manifest.json`, `claims.md`, `metrics.json`, `results.md`; none exist yet.
## Reuse
Share time-alignment, economics, source-rights checks and synthetic fixtures with other QMG skills, but keep the domain-specific model scoped here.
