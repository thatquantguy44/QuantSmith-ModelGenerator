# SOURCES — Collateral Reuse Chain Stress Testing
## Open/public starting points (official verified portals)
- [BIS research and statistics](https://www.bis.org/) — verified public institutional landing page, NOT proof of an available granular series.
- [New York Fed SOFR data](https://www.newyorkfed.org/markets/reference-rates/sofr) — verified public institutional landing page, NOT proof of an available granular series.
These are **context/source entry points**, not confirmation that Source provenance, permissioned reuse contracts, collateral movements, haircut and margin events is available for download at required precision.
## Licensed / commercial
- licensed settlement asset characteristics and market haircuts. Inspect license, as-of coverage, revisions, permissible uses, price, and redistribution restrictions.
## Permissioned internal, not to be committed
- approved collateral lot provenance and reuse restrictions. May only be accessed in an authorized private environment, with anonymization and entitlement controls.
## Source diligence
Build a data dictionary for Source provenance, permissioned reuse contracts, collateral movements, haircut and margin events, with effective time vs published time, market clock, methodology, survivorship and audit citation. Add verified DOI links for relevant academic papers when discovered; none have been certified in this initial batch.
