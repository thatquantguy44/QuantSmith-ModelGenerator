# Reusable skill — Collateral Reuse Chain Stress Testing
## Trigger
When deciding how to **set buffers and resilience policies for eligible collateral transformation chains**.
## Preconditions
Authoritative SPEC/PLAN, confirmed timestamp and unit, authorized access to Source provenance, permissioned reuse contracts, collateral movements, haircut and margin events, approved risk and cost model.
## Method
1. Restate hypothesis and observable decision.
2. Build immutable source and licensing ledger; reject inaccessible private data.
3. Create point-in-time synthetic fixture for collateral-reuse-stress; forbid future updates.
4. Rebuild gross collateral stock and static stress with real costs and legal constraints.
5. Specify legal reuse network input-output simulation, then optionally dynamic network cascades with haircuts and intraday liquidity.
6. Run time-split/paired holdout evaluation: capacity contraction, margin calls, recovery time and chain failure.
7. Stress primary threat: double counting and unsupported rehypothecation rights.
8. Write falsification, negative findings and exact run manifest.
9. Update HANDOFF truthfully; request human risk and economic review.
## Output
Proposed `run_manifest.json`, `claims.md`, `metrics.json`, `results.md`; none exist yet.
## Reuse
Share time-alignment, economics, source-rights checks and synthetic fixtures with other QMG skills, but keep the domain-specific model scoped here.
