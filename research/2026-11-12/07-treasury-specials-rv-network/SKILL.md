# Reusable skill — Treasury Specials Relative-Value Network
## Trigger
When deciding how to **identify executable repo RV across related Treasury CUSIPs**.
## Preconditions
Authoritative SPEC/PLAN, confirmed timestamp and unit, authorized access to CUSIP-level repo and general-collateral rates, SOMA auctions, supply, futures basket, fails, approved risk and cost model.
## Method
1. Restate hypothesis and observable decision.
2. Build immutable source and licensing ledger; reject inaccessible private data.
3. Create point-in-time synthetic fixture for treasury-specials-rv-network; forbid future updates.
4. Rebuild historical CUSIP z-score of specials with real costs and legal constraints.
5. Specify CUSIP graph, cointegration and structural event controls, then optionally dynamic security-specific financing equilibrium.
6. Run time-split/paired holdout evaluation: net spread convergence, realized returns, capital and fail risk.
7. Stress primary threat: public SOMA cannot substitute for executable CUSIP quotes.
8. Write falsification, negative findings and exact run manifest.
9. Update HANDOFF truthfully; request human risk and economic review.
## Output
Proposed `run_manifest.json`, `claims.md`, `metrics.json`, `results.md`; none exist yet.

Share time-alignment, economics, source-rights checks and synthetic fixtures with other QMG skills, but keep the domain-specific model scoped here.
