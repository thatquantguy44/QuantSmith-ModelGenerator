# Research skill — Global Shipping Connectivity Re-Routing to Inflation and Equity Margins
## Trigger
When a quant team asks to **Size logistics/inflation hedges when public route connectivity and port-service centrality shift.** in East Asia–Europe–US maritime routes, transport/industrial equity sectors.
## Required inputs
Legal scope, decision timestamp, country/port route × monthly index release × subsequent inflation/earnings outcome, fields (UNCTAD monthly/quarterly liner connectivity, MARAD freight/port dashboards, shipping rate indexes where licensed, CPI/PPI and issuer logistics costs.), approved source license, trade/operating costs and untouched outcome window.
## Steps
1. Inspect SPEC H1/H0 and Batch-14 trade-network export shock model uses reported trade value; this studies route topology and alternate maritime connectivity.
2. Cite official source links, separate publication from effective date; follow UNCTAD latest confirmed index data update Sep 28 2026; a 2026-11-20 decision cannot use December data.
3. Build a synthetic point-in-time fixture with missing, late and contradictory events.
4. Reproduce baseline `Aggregate global shipping index versus region CPI with fixed lag`, compare to do-nothing under matched constraints.
5. Prototype `Supply-route network exposure local projections` and retain `Causal graph shock transmission with endogenous alternative route capacity and measurement error` only if ablation justifies.
6. Evaluate `inflation nowcast gain, equity margin dispersion, attribution stability and hedged net P&L`; stress Connectivity is not real-time port congestion and quarterly updates can obscure freight cycle timing.
7. Apply hard stop: No incremental signal after global industrial production, oil and published trade volume controls.
8. Report cost/capacity and explicit invented ROI sensitivity separately from verified empirical effects.
9. Update HANDOFF with evidence links and red flags; no live integration without human signoff.
## Output contract
Proposed `source_ledger.json`, `pit_manifest.json`, `baseline_metrics.json`, `candidate_metrics.json`, `risk_report.md`, `review_record.md` (not created by this doc PR).
## Failure behavior
Missing data rights or no feasible comparator → BLOCKED; do not fabricate a substitution, source license or outcome.
