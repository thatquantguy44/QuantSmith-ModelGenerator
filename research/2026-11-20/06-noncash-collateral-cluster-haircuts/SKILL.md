# Research skill — Noncash Collateral Correlation-Cluster Haircut Controller
## Trigger
When a quant team asks to **Recommend minimum defensive haircuts for a collateral basket based on joint concentration and liquidation correlation.** in Multi-asset collateral supporting US/EU securities loans.
## Required inputs
Legal scope, decision timestamp, collateral security × issuer cluster × loan exposure × as-of time, fields (Approved collateral constituents, synthetic issuer cross-holdings, stressed factor returns, liquidation bid/ask, haircut and legal threshold.), approved source license, trade/operating costs and untouched outcome window.
## Steps
1. Inspect SPEC H1/H0 and Not stale-mark quarantine (price integrity) or wrong-way *borrower default* risk: chooses collateral cluster haircut policy at portfolio level.
2. Cite official source links, separate publication from effective date; follow Apply only return histories, prices, group linkages and issuer memberships known at the decision timestamp.
3. Build a synthetic point-in-time fixture with missing, late and contradictory events.
4. Reproduce baseline `Uniform issuer/asset-class static haircut`, compare to do-nothing under matched constraints.
5. Prototype `Scenario CVaR optimization with issuer cluster caps` and retain `Distributionally robust ambiguity-set CVaR with wrong-way liquidity correlation` only if ablation justifies.
6. Evaluate `shortfall ES, collateral usage, haircut turnover, stress exceptions`; stress Asset covariance may break exactly when haircuts are needed and public free-float proxies omit liquidation controls.
7. Apply hard stop: Risk benefit disappears when using stressed-correlation holdouts or violates legal haircut floors.
8. Report cost/capacity and explicit invented ROI sensitivity separately from verified empirical effects.
9. Update HANDOFF with evidence links and red flags; no live integration without human signoff.
## Output contract
Proposed `source_ledger.json`, `pit_manifest.json`, `baseline_metrics.json`, `candidate_metrics.json`, `risk_report.md`, `review_record.md` (not created by this doc PR).
## Failure behavior
Missing data rights or no feasible comparator → BLOCKED; do not fabricate a substitution, source license or outcome.
