# Research skill — Cyber-Outage Contingent Repo Funding Recovery Routes
## Trigger
When a quant team asks to **Pre-approve alternate cash-lender/clearing routes and reserve a minimum contingency liquidity buffer for an exogenous operational outage.** in US cleared and tri-party repo.
## Required inputs
Legal scope, decision timestamp, route × lender class × hourly outage scenario, fields (Timestamped anonymized funding route uptime, fallback approvals, cutoff times, public OFR repo volumes/rates, synthetic outage schedules.), approved source license, trade/operating costs and untouched outcome window.
## Steps
1. Inspect SPEC H1/H0 and Distinct from batch-15 benchmark-outage monitoring: this optimizes funding continuity under cyber node failure, not price publication.
2. Cite official source links, separate publication from effective date; follow OFR cleared rate/volume normally T+1 and tri-party T+2, so use public series as lagged calibration only; route outage telemetry must be timestamped pre-decision.
3. Build a synthetic point-in-time fixture with missing, late and contradictory events.
4. Reproduce baseline `Fixed 10% contingency funding buffer with one permitted fallback`, compare to do-nothing under matched constraints.
5. Prototype `Two-stage robust multi-commodity flow with cyber failure scenarios` and retain `Distributionally robust scenario-tree route control with simultaneous correlated cash-lender disruptions` only if ablation justifies.
6. Evaluate `shortfall dollars, p95 duration, liquidity carry, fail probability, recovery cost`; stress Public aggregates cannot recover the bank-level cash-lender network or prove an actual route is legally available.
7. Apply hard stop: Held-out synthetic/approved outage replay fails to reduce mean + tail shortfall at fixed total contingency cost.
8. Report cost/capacity and explicit invented ROI sensitivity separately from verified empirical effects.
9. Update HANDOFF with evidence links and red flags; no live integration without human signoff.
## Output contract
Proposed `source_ledger.json`, `pit_manifest.json`, `baseline_metrics.json`, `candidate_metrics.json`, `risk_report.md`, `review_record.md` (not created by this doc PR).
## Failure behavior
Missing data rights or no feasible comparator → BLOCKED; do not fabricate a substitution, source license or outcome.
