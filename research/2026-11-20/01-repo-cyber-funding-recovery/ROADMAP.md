# ROADMAP — Cyber-Outage Contingent Repo Funding Recovery Routes
**Phase 0: source and math validation (weeks 1–2).** Confirm US cleared and tri-party repo, allowable decision Pre-approve alternate cash-lender/clearing routes and reserve a minimum contingency liquidity buffer for an exogenous operational outage., units in `min_{x,b} E_s[ FundingGap_s(x,b)*p_s + SwitchingCost_s(x) ] + CarryCost(b)`, executable baseline and exact publication lag. **Gate:** legal/data entitlements and H0 agreed.

**Phase 1: synthetic engineering (weeks 3–4).** Implement typed route × lender class × hourly outage scenario fixture, deterministic Fixed 10% contingency funding buffer with one permitted fallback and source-vintage checksum. **Gate:** AC-001 and AC-002, fail-closed data access.

**Phase 2: empirical research (weeks 5–8).** If data licensed, compare Two-stage robust multi-commodity flow with cyber failure scenarios versus holdout baseline; optionally prototype Distributionally robust scenario-tree route control with simultaneous correlated cash-lender disruptions. **Gate:** Held-out synthetic/approved outage replay fails to reduce mean + tail shortfall at fixed total contingency cost.

**Phase 3: robustness (weeks 9–12).** Stress Public aggregates cannot recover the bank-level cash-lender network or prove an actual route is legally available.; evaluate shortfall dollars, p95 duration, liquidity carry, fail probability, recovery cost, capacity, capital, transaction costs and estimation uncertainty. **Gate:** report both negative and positive scenarios.

**Phase 4: read-only shadow (conditional).** Human-reviewed monitoring and rollback, no automatic trading. **Current state:** no phase has started; schedule estimates are planning assumptions.
