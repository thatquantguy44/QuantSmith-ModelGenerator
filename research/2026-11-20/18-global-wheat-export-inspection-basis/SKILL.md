# Research skill — Wheat Export Inspection Revisions to Global Basis and Calendar Spreads
## Trigger
When a quant team asks to **Hedge wheat calendar spreads around official export inspection versus world balance-sheet information releases.** in US, Black Sea and international wheat futures/physical flows.
## Required inputs
Legal scope, decision timestamp, weekly USDA inspection release × grain class × destination × exchange quote timestamp, fields (USDA AMS export grain inspections dashboard, USDA wheat supply/price tables, official crop calendars, futures curves and export policy announcement records.), approved source license, trade/operating costs and untouched outcome window.
## Steps
1. Inspect SPEC H1/H0 and Unlike Australian water allocation/crop basis, uses multinational export timing, inspection vintages and wheat calendar spreads.
2. Cite official source links, separate publication from effective date; follow USDA dashboard launched Oct 14 2026 and wheat tables updated Nov 12 2026; do not replace historic reports with latest annual revisions.
3. Build a synthetic point-in-time fixture with missing, late and contradictory events.
4. Reproduce baseline `Monthly seasonal wheat spread regression using last known WASDE`, compare to do-nothing under matched constraints.
5. Prototype `Mixed-frequency distributed-lag export surprise model` and retain `Structural Bayesian crop-flow network with regional stock constraints and censoring` only if ablation justifies.
6. Evaluate `spread forecast delta, regional basis tracking, net slippage-adjusted hedge P&L, regime robustness`; stress Weekly inspections differ from actual export commitments; Black Sea supply and policy announcements may dominate.
7. Apply hard stop: No predictive improvement after weather, futures calendar effects and contemporaneous official release lags.
8. Report cost/capacity and explicit invented ROI sensitivity separately from verified empirical effects.
9. Update HANDOFF with evidence links and red flags; no live integration without human signoff.
## Output contract
Proposed `source_ledger.json`, `pit_manifest.json`, `baseline_metrics.json`, `candidate_metrics.json`, `risk_report.md`, `review_record.md` (not created by this doc PR).
## Failure behavior
Missing data rights or no feasible comparator → BLOCKED; do not fabricate a substitution, source license or outcome.
