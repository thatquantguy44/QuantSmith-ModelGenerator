## No-lookahead checklist
Before model training, require `available_at_utc`, `observed_at_utc`, `effective_at_utc`, `retrieved_at_utc`, immutable source revision, identifier, license and a market calendar. Where no historic publication tape exists, prevent favorable backtest claims and use synthetic fixture evaluation only.
