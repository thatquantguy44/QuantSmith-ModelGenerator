# Research skill — Tokenized Treasury Collateral DvP Finality and Fallback Stress
## Trigger
When a quant team asks to **Select permitted collateral settlement pathway with provable delivery-versus-payment, finality and fail-safe fallback.** in US Treasury collateral, DTC-tokenized entitlements and conventional settlement.
## Required inputs
Legal scope, decision timestamp, collateral lot × settlement leg × wallet/ledger state × timestamp, fields (DTCC published service documentation, synthetic token mint-burn and conventional DvP events, validation proofs, cutoff windows and rehypothecation eligibility.), approved source license, trade/operating costs and untouched outcome window.
## Steps
1. Inspect SPEC H1/H0 and No prior batch models token entitlement lifecycle or cross-ledger DvP/finality; not equivalent to calendar bridging or cross-book identity.
2. Cite official source links, separate publication from effective date; follow Use current production DTS service scope as of actual published date; planned 2027 integrations are NOT usable at a 2026 decision time.
3. Build a synthetic point-in-time fixture with missing, late and contradictory events.
4. Reproduce baseline `Conventional permitted book-entry DvP with settlement cutoff`, compare to do-nothing under matched constraints.
5. Prototype `Timed Petri net state-machine model of mint/transfer/burn and escrow/finality rules` and retain `Formal model checking of cross-system DvP atomicity with stochastic disruption transitions` only if ablation justifies.
6. Evaluate `liquidity-hours locked, failed atomicity scenarios, rollback time, eligible-settlement capacity`; stress Token entitlements must not be treated as interchangeable with every conventional collateral/clearing agreement.
7. Apply hard stop: Cannot demonstrate legal/technical atomicity on synthetic fail-mid-transfer tests, or route fails to save liquidity net of controls.
8. Report cost/capacity and explicit invented ROI sensitivity separately from verified empirical effects.
9. Update HANDOFF with evidence links and red flags; no live integration without human signoff.
## Output contract
Proposed `source_ledger.json`, `pit_manifest.json`, `baseline_metrics.json`, `candidate_metrics.json`, `risk_report.md`, `review_record.md` (not created by this doc PR).
## Failure behavior
Missing data rights or no feasible comparator → BLOCKED; do not fabricate a substitution, source license or outcome.
