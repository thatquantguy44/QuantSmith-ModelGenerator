# Research skill — Public BDC Portfolio Fair-Value Markdown Lead Indicator
## Trigger
When a quant team asks to **Prioritize due diligence or credit hedges where public business development company holdings imply delayed loan fair-value marks.** in US public BDC equities, leveraged loans and credit sentiment.
## Required inputs
Legal scope, decision timestamp, BDC filing × portfolio company/sector × disclosure timestamp, fields (Original SEC BDC 10-Q/10-K investment schedules and mark values, sector classification, publicly known credit outcomes and licensed leveraged-loan pricing.), approved source license, trade/operating costs and untouched outcome window.
## Steps
1. Inspect SPEC H1/H0 and Distinct from CMBS maturity/refinance hazard and TRACE ETF NAV stress: tests *BDC marked private loan holdings*.
2. Cite official source links, separate publication from effective date; follow Use filed investment schedules only after filing acceptance; amendments/revised portfolio marks cannot enter historical quarters.
3. Build a synthetic point-in-time fixture with missing, late and contradictory events.
4. Reproduce baseline `BDC aggregate NAV discount and published nonaccrual percentage`, compare to do-nothing under matched constraints.
5. Prototype `Issuer-level mark-hazard panel with censored holdings and missing-not-at-random controls` and retain `Hierarchical Bayesian marked-to-market credit migration model with portfolio network dependence` only if ablation justifies.
6. Evaluate `future credit-event AUC/PR, mark-calibration error, NAV stress accuracy, opportunity capacity`; stress BDC portfolio company identities, valuation methods and distressed sales are not uniformly disclosed; matching bias.
7. Apply hard stop: No improvement over current NAV discount/nonaccrual baseline in later quarters or effects only known after financial reports.
8. Report cost/capacity and explicit invented ROI sensitivity separately from verified empirical effects.
9. Update HANDOFF with evidence links and red flags; no live integration without human signoff.
## Output contract
Proposed `source_ledger.json`, `pit_manifest.json`, `baseline_metrics.json`, `candidate_metrics.json`, `risk_report.md`, `review_record.md` (not created by this doc PR).
## Failure behavior
Missing data rights or no feasible comparator → BLOCKED; do not fabricate a substitution, source license or outcome.
