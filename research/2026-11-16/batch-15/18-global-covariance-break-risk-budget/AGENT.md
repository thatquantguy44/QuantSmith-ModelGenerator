# Research-agent contract — Global Covariance-Break Risk-Budget Controller

**Authority:** analyze US, Europe, Japan and liquid EM; equity, rates, FX and commodities using public, rights-approved or synthetic inputs; propose experiments and auditable recommendations. This contract does not instantiate an agent executor. Do not trade, message counterparties, repricing-contracts, certify legal rights, grant data access, deploy or merge.

**Inputs:** Daily index/futures/ETF total returns and FX, point-in-time instrument starts/ends, roll calendars, trading sessions, bid/ask and cost curves. H.15 can support rate diagnostics but is not a global total-return panel. Require `instrument x completed local session x common information cutoff` plus source/version/hash, unit/currency and the common time envelope. Public run mode rejects permissioned adapters and vendor extracts whose redistribution has not been approved.

**Decision scope:** Choose between holding current risk weights and a constrained rebalance when covariance-change evidence exceeds its execution cost. Build lag-aware covariance, break statistic and marginal turnover cost, compare baseline `Monthly risk parity using 60-day EWMA covariance and fixed weight caps; compare a simple shrinkage covariance with no break detection.` with `Sequential covariance change detector plus shrinkage and cost-aware no-trade bands`, and return hold/rebalance research decision, risk-contribution certificate and stale-session report. Preserve failed fits and HOLD/BLOCKED actions.

**Evidence gates:** distinguish FACT (a linked source supports precisely the stated claim), HYPOTHESIS (the new research question), ASSUMPTION (cost/capacity/timing), CALCULATION (explicit inputs/formula) and UNVERIFIED. A paper or matching citation span cannot establish a new empirical effect. No statement of outperformance is permitted before [EVALS](EVALS.md) and QMG-20261116-18-AC-01 pass.

**Hard controls:** Decide at 16:30 America/New_York using only completed market sessions and public data received by then; execute each market's next liquid session. Stale Asian observations remain lagged rather than filled with later prices. All returns carry local session end and actual receive time; no forward fill across a market closure without a stale flag. Delay Japanese returns one extra session and perturb calendar alignment: claimed benefit should remain directionally stable; future-price replacement must fail an availability assertion.

**Escalation:** missing rights, contradictory terms, unknown availability or infeasibility => BLOCKED with affected IDs and minimal reproducible evidence. Economics review uses the README ROI formula; never fill missing prices with synthetic values without explicit synthetic labeling. Human domain reviewer assesses Look-ahead session alignment, ETF backfill, futures rolls and survivorship change the correlation structure. Same-day cross-market fills may be economically impossible.
