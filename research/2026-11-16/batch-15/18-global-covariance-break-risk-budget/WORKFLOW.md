# Research workflow — Global Covariance-Break Risk-Budget Controller

1. **Data => rights/time audit.** Ingest instrument x completed local session x common information cutoff through proposed `global_session_returns`; save exact release bytes privately where rights permit and only public-safe manifests here. Check Decide at 16:30 America/New_York using only completed market sessions and public data received by then; execute each market's next liquid session. Stale Asian observations remain lagged rather than filled with later prices. Unknown data rights or availability yields BLOCKED.
2. **Hypothesis => pre-registration.** Freeze Sequential break detection with a no-trade region lowers realized risk-budget tracking error by at least 15% versus monthly EWMA rebalancing without increasing turnover. Define native-horizon targets and independent event groups; lock costs, limits and partition dates before seeing holdout outcomes.
3. **Baseline => reproducible control.** Implement Monthly risk parity using 60-day EWMA covariance and fixed weight caps; compare a simple shrinkage covariance with no break detection. Verify Delay Japanese returns one extra session and perturb calendar alignment: claimed benefit should remain directionally stable; future-price replacement must fail an availability assertion. Keep equal information and economic constraints across methods.
4. **Candidate => interpretable action.** Build lag-aware covariance, break statistic and marginal turnover cost; fit/solve Sequential covariance change detector plus shrinkage and cost-aware no-trade bands. Return hold/rebalance research decision, risk-contribution certificate and stale-session report plus a feasibility/time certificate. Advanced research `Distributionally robust risk-budget optimization over post-break covariance ambiguity sets` stays optional.
5. **Validation => objective verdict.** Report risk-budget RMSE, realized volatility overshoot, net return, drawdown, turnover and alarm false positives. GO if risk-budget RMSE falls >=15% and turnover does not rise over >=3 held-out regimes and multiple asynchronous-market assumptions; STOP if improvement is due to more leverage, reduced target risk or tuned crisis dates. Double execution costs and halve capacity; include zero-benefit and delayed-launch ROI scenarios.
6. **Review => private shadow.** Domain reviewer challenges Look-ahead session alignment, ETF backfill, futures rolls and survivorship change the correlation structure. Same-day cross-market fills may be economically impossible. Complete rights and capacity evidence before any prospective shadow run. Human review is required; runtime deployment and trading need separate authority.

All transitions are future gates, currently BLOCKED. A failed experiment is retained with source/config/code hashes; reruns cannot overwrite an adverse outcome or silently relax a threshold. Generated reports label FACT/HYPOTHESIS/ASSUMPTION/CALCULATION/UNVERIFIED and preserve the original baseline alongside candidate output.
