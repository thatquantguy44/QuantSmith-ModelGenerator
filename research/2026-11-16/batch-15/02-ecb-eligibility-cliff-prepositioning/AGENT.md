# Research-agent contract — ECB Collateral Eligibility-Cliff Prepositioning

**Authority:** analyze Euro area; EUR marketable collateral using public, rights-approved or synthetic inputs; propose experiments and auditable recommendations. This contract does not instantiate an agent executor. Do not trade, message counterparties, repricing-contracts, certify legal rights, grant data access, deploy or merge.

**Inputs:** ECB full/delta eligibility files, announcement timestamps, effective dates, maturity, haircuts, issuer mapping, replacement prices and settlement windows. Do not infer a future delisting from a later archive snapshot. Require `ISIN x release version x effective date` plus source/version/hash, unit/currency and the common time envelope. Public run mode rejects permissioned adapters and vendor extracts whose redistribution has not been approved.

**Decision scope:** Preposition replacement assets before an announced effective date while charging carry, haircut and transfer costs. Build announced future eligibility matrix, haircut-adjusted coverage and replacement ladder, compare baseline `Replace only at maturity or first effective ineligibility date using cheapest currently eligible asset.` with `Rolling-horizon linear allocation using announced eligibility transitions`, and return dated replacement schedule and coverage stress certificate. Preserve failed fits and HOLD/BLOCKED actions.

**Evidence gates:** distinguish FACT (a linked source supports precisely the stated claim), HYPOTHESIS (the new research question), ASSUMPTION (cost/capacity/timing), CALCULATION (explicit inputs/formula) and UNVERIFIED. A paper or matching citation span cannot establish a new empirical effect. No statement of outperformance is permitted before [EVALS](EVALS.md) and QMG-20261116-02-AC-01 pass.

**Hard controls:** Evaluate after verified ECB publication and ingestion, with a minimum 30-minute operational latency; any action is next eligible settlement window. Historical daily snapshots without timestamp proof are usable only with a conservative next-business-day decision lag. Preserve source byte hashes and schema versions; reject any unknown CHANGE code or missing effective date instead of guessing. Move an announcement after the decision timestamp: all corresponding anticipatory trades must disappear; maturity may remain predictable from previously published terms.

**Escalation:** missing rights, contradictory terms, unknown availability or infeasibility => BLOCKED with affected IDs and minimal reproducible evidence. Economics review uses the README ROI formula; never fill missing prices with synthetic values without explicit synthetic labeling. Human domain reviewer assesses Current eligibility is not historical eligibility; rating changes may be anticipated in prices. Framework eligibility does not establish institution-specific usability, own-use treatment or transfer rights.
