# Research workflow — ECB Collateral Eligibility-Cliff Prepositioning

1. **Data => rights/time audit.** Ingest ISIN x release version x effective date through proposed `ecb_eligibility`; save exact release bytes privately where rights permit and only public-safe manifests here. Check Evaluate after verified ECB publication and ingestion, with a minimum 30-minute operational latency; any action is next eligible settlement window. Historical daily snapshots without timestamp proof are usable only with a conservative next-business-day decision lag. Unknown data rights or availability yields BLOCKED.
2. **Hypothesis => pre-registration.** Freeze A versioned effective-date planner lowers replacement expense by at least 2 annualized basis points on affected balances versus replacing at the deadline, with no use of information before announcement. Define native-horizon targets and independent event groups; lock costs, limits and partition dates before seeing holdout outcomes.
3. **Baseline => reproducible control.** Implement Replace only at maturity or first effective ineligibility date using cheapest currently eligible asset. Verify Move an announcement after the decision timestamp: all corresponding anticipatory trades must disappear; maturity may remain predictable from previously published terms. Keep equal information and economic constraints across methods.
4. **Candidate => interpretable action.** Build announced future eligibility matrix, haircut-adjusted coverage and replacement ladder; fit/solve Rolling-horizon linear allocation using announced eligibility transitions. Return dated replacement schedule and coverage stress certificate plus a feasibility/time certificate. Advanced research `Distributionally robust prepositioning under uncertain unannounced eligibility loss` stays optional.
5. **Validation => objective verdict.** Report net carry/transfer expense, shortfall, false early replacements, turnover and issuer concentration. GO if cost saving >=2 annualized bps on affected balances and zero eligibility violations across >=20 independent transition clusters; worst stress shortfall no larger than baseline. STOP if timestamp-safe sample is inadequate or cost rises under doubled bid/ask. Double execution costs and halve capacity; include zero-benefit and delayed-launch ROI scenarios.
6. **Review => private shadow.** Domain reviewer challenges Current eligibility is not historical eligibility; rating changes may be anticipated in prices. Framework eligibility does not establish institution-specific usability, own-use treatment or transfer rights. Complete rights and capacity evidence before any prospective shadow run. Human review is required; runtime deployment and trading need separate authority.

All transitions are future gates, currently BLOCKED. A failed experiment is retained with source/config/code hashes; reruns cannot overwrite an adverse outcome or silently relax a threshold. Generated reports label FACT/HYPOTHESIS/ASSUMPTION/CALCULATION/UNVERIFIED and preserve the original baseline alongside candidate output.
