# Roadmap — ECB Collateral Eligibility-Cliff Prepositioning

**Effort assumption:** One researcher plus one data engineer for 6-10 weeks before a private execution pilot. Elapsed time and ROI are conditional, not commitments.

1. **G0 — Evidence and rights (BLOCKED).** Resolve the ranked ladder in SOURCES. Obtain a timestamp/units sample for ISIN x release version x effective date; identify incomplete public coverage. STOP if required economic targets or first-public times cannot be recovered legally.
2. **G1 — Synthetic contract proof (BLOCKED on G0).** Implement the as-of envelope, announced future eligibility matrix, haircut-adjusted coverage and replacement ladder, and independent negative fixture: Move an announcement after the decision timestamp: all corresponding anticipatory trades must disappear; maturity may remain predictable from previously published terms. GO only with zero future-row acceptance and explicit fallback behavior.
3. **G2 — Credible baseline (BLOCKED on G1).** Implement Replace only at maturity or first effective ineligibility date using cheapest currently eligible asset. with actual or clearly labeled scenario costs. Freeze target horizon, cohorts, primary metric and parameters before holdout. Pin dependency hashes and data manifests.
4. **G3 — Candidate challenge (BLOCKED on G2).** Compare Rolling-horizon linear allocation using announced eligibility transitions to baseline, with Distributionally robust prepositioning under uncertain unannounced eligibility loss reserved as an optional later branch. GO if cost saving >=2 annualized bps on affected balances and zero eligibility violations across >=20 independent transition clusters; worst stress shortfall no larger than baseline. STOP if timestamp-safe sample is inadequate or cost rises under doubled bid/ask.
5. **G4 — Capacity and shadow (BLOCKED on G3).** Verify Illustrative EUR500m affected annual balances, converted to USD equivalent for ROI. Limited by replacement-asset depth, settlement access and concentration limits; use <=1% observed daily turnover until calibrated. At least 60 eligible decisions and 90 calendar days, or longer to include a full native seasonal/event cycle, are required before production review. Rarity-driven insufficient samples remain BLOCKED; synthetic shocks cannot count as real economic observations.
6. **G5 — Production review (BLOCKED on G4).** Reconcile dated replacement schedule and coverage stress certificate with a private independently maintained reference, confirm operational ownership and rollback, then request separate deployment/trading authority. Any hard-constraint breach halts the pilot.

Release the simplest model that passes all gates; abandon if measured break-even annual benefit from README cannot be justified. A statistically interesting negative result may still support publication: Medium-high: event-time collateral option value, with public framework changes and transparent effective-time tests.
