# Research-agent contract — European Gas Storage Deliverability Hedge

**Authority:** analyze Germany, Netherlands, Italy and European gas hubs using public, rights-approved or synthetic inputs; propose experiments and auditable recommendations. This contract does not instantiate an agent executor. Do not trade, message counterparties, repricing-contracts, certify legal rights, grant data access, deploy or merge.

**Inputs:** GIE published facility stocks/capacities/data-quality flags, publication versions, hub connectivity and outage notices; inventory-dependent performance curves need validated operator data or explicitly bounded assumptions. Licensed day-ahead/futures curves for hedging. Require `storage facility x gas day x publication version` plus source/version/hash, unit/currency and the common time envelope. Public run mode rejects permissioned adapters and vendor extracts whose redistribution has not been approved.

**Decision scope:** Hedge winter peak gas-delivery risk using facility-level withdrawal constraints rather than national percent-full alone. Build withdrawal headroom, gas-day inventory state and hub connectivity, compare baseline `Hedge ratio proportional to national percent-full deficit relative to trailing seasonal average.` with `Constrained physical storage network and linear hedge projection`, and return deliverability risk index and constrained seasonal hedge weights. Preserve failed fits and HOLD/BLOCKED actions.

**Evidence gates:** distinguish FACT (a linked source supports precisely the stated claim), HYPOTHESIS (the new research question), ASSUMPTION (cost/capacity/timing), CALCULATION (explicit inputs/formula) and UNVERIFIED. A paper or matching citation span cannot establish a new empirical effect. No statement of outperformance is permitted before [EVALS](EVALS.md) and QMG-20261116-13-AC-01 pass.

**Hard controls:** Recompute after first verified daily publication; execute next liquid hub session with at least one-day lag when historical publication time is unknown. Revised storage values never replace first-release features. Use gas-day definitions and MWh conversion consistently; data-quality flags propagate to bounds instead of becoming zero inventory. Create two facilities with identical percent-full and different withdrawal limits: shortage outputs must differ. Remove one interconnector and forbid unphysical gas flows.

**Escalation:** missing rights, contradictory terms, unknown availability or infeasibility => BLOCKED with affected IDs and minimal reproducible evidence. Economics review uses the README ROI formula; never fill missing prices with synthetic values without explicit synthetic labeling. Human domain reviewer assesses Reported maximum withdrawal is not necessarily sustainable at low fill. Regional bottlenecks and revisions dominate; proxy futures do not deliver facility access.
