5. **Validation => objective verdict.** Report procurement CVaR, physical shortfall, hedge cost, basis risk, coverage and sensitivity to withdrawal curves. GO if >=10% CVaR reduction at equal mean cost across >=5 winters including one held-out stressed winter; STOP if results reverse under plausible deliverability curves or cross-border restrictions. Double execution costs and halve capacity; include zero-benefit and delayed-launch ROI scenarios.

Regional bottlenecks and revisions dominate; proxy futures do not deliver facility access. Complete rights and capacity evidence before any prospective shadow run. Human review is required; runtime deployment and trading need separate authority.
