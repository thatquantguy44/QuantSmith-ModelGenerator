# Roadmap — European Gas Storage Deliverability Hedge

**Effort assumption:** One energy researcher and one engineer for 10-14 weeks. Elapsed time and ROI are conditional, not commitments.

1. **G0 — Evidence and rights (BLOCKED).** Resolve the ranked ladder in SOURCES. Obtain a timestamp/units sample for storage facility x gas day x publication version; identify incomplete public coverage. STOP if required economic targets or first-public times cannot be recovered legally.
2. **G1 — Synthetic contract proof (BLOCKED on G0).** Implement the as-of envelope, withdrawal headroom, gas-day inventory state and hub connectivity, and independent negative fixture: Create two facilities with identical percent-full and different withdrawal limits: shortage outputs must differ. Remove one interconnector and forbid unphysical gas flows. GO only with zero future-row acceptance and explicit fallback behavior.
3. **G2 — Credible baseline (BLOCKED on G1).** Implement Hedge ratio proportional to national percent-full deficit relative to trailing seasonal average. with actual or clearly labeled scenario costs. Freeze target horizon, cohorts, primary metric and parameters before holdout. Pin dependency hashes and data manifests.
4. **G3 — Candidate challenge (BLOCKED on G2).** Compare Constrained physical storage network and linear hedge projection to baseline, with Stochastic dynamic storage model with uncertain withdrawal curves and robust hub hedge allocation reserved as an optional later branch. GO if >=10% CVaR reduction at equal mean cost across >=5 winters including one held-out stressed winter; STOP if results reverse under plausible deliverability curves or cross-border restrictions.
5. **G4 — Capacity and shadow (BLOCKED on G3).** Verify Illustrative $30m annual hedge exposure; use <=1% lagged hub/spread volume and explicit spread liquidity. USD ROI translates net EUR procurement savings at hedged rates; no assumed storage ownership. At least 60 eligible decisions and 90 calendar days, or longer to include a full native seasonal/event cycle, are required before production review. Rarity-driven insufficient samples remain BLOCKED; synthetic shocks cannot count as real economic observations.
6. **G5 — Production review (BLOCKED on G4).** Reconcile deliverability risk index and constrained seasonal hedge weights with a private independently maintained reference, confirm operational ownership and rollback, then request separate deployment/trading authority. Any hard-constraint breach halts the pilot.

Release the simplest model that passes all gates; abandon if measured break-even annual benefit from README cannot be justified. A statistically interesting negative result may still support publication: High: physical deliverability versus headline inventory; falsifiable nonlinear constraints rather than another weather predictor.
