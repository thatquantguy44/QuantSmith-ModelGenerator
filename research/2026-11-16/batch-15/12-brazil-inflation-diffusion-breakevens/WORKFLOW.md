# Research workflow — Brazil Inflation Diffusion versus Breakeven Carry

1. **Data => rights/time audit.** Ingest inflation release x survey public version x matched bond pair through proposed `brazil_inflation_events`; save exact release bytes privately where rights permit and only public-safe manifests here. Check Trade no earlier than next Sao Paulo session after verified inflation and survey publication, with one-hour processing lag. Survey collection dates are never treated as public timestamps. Unknown data rights or availability yields BLOCKED.
2. **Hypothesis => pre-registration.** Freeze Inflation-breadth revisions and Focus dispersion improve next-quarter breakeven return forecasts by at least 10% squared-error reduction versus carry plus headline surprise, and add 20 annualized net bps at fixed risk. Define native-horizon targets and independent event groups; lock costs, limits and partition dates before seeing holdout outcomes.
3. **Baseline => reproducible control.** Implement Carry/roll plus headline inflation surprise using a fixed ridge regression and identical risk budget. Verify Set nominal and linked yields to parallel shifts with no inflation shock: residual P&L must reflect declared duration mismatch, not be mislabeled inflation alpha. Keep equal information and economic constraints across methods.
4. **Candidate => interpretable action.** Build released inflation diffusion, survey dispersion and known indexation carry; fit/solve Sparse distributed-lag regression with local-projection diagnostics. Return breakeven forecast, DV01 hedge and market-access gate plus a feasibility/time certificate. Advanced research `Bayesian inflation component state-space model with scenario-robust breakeven sizing` stays optional.
5. **Validation => objective verdict.** Report out-of-sample MSE, net bps, real/nominal hedge residual, turnover, drawdown and calibration across inflation regimes. GO if >=10% forecast MSE improvement and >=20 net annualized bps at unchanged risk with >=40 nonoverlapping quarterly outcomes; STOP if official component vintage or executable curve history cannot be established. Double execution costs and halve capacity; include zero-benefit and delayed-launch ROI scenarios.
6. **Review => private shadow.** Domain reviewer challenges Overlapping quarterly labels create false sample size; inflation indexation/tax rules and market-access costs can erase apparent returns. No current Brazilian tax recommendation is assumed. Complete rights and capacity evidence before any prospective shadow run. Human review is required; runtime deployment and trading need separate authority.

All transitions are future gates, currently BLOCKED. A failed experiment is retained with source/config/code hashes; reruns cannot overwrite an adverse outcome or silently relax a threshold. Generated reports label FACT/HYPOTHESIS/ASSUMPTION/CALCULATION/UNVERIFIED and preserve the original baseline alongside candidate output.
