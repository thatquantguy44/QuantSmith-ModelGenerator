# Roadmap — Treasury Futures Delivery Inventory Reservation

**Effort assumption:** One derivatives researcher and one engineer for 12-16 weeks. Elapsed time and ROI are conditional, not commitments.

1. **G0 — Evidence and rights (BLOCKED).** Resolve the ranked ladder in SOURCES. Obtain a timestamp/units sample for CUSIP x futures contract x observation timestamp; identify incomplete public coverage. STOP if required economic targets or first-public times cannot be recovered legally.
2. **G1 — Synthetic contract proof (BLOCKED on G0).** Implement the as-of envelope, invoice value, basket eligibility and marginal delivery option shadow cost, and independent negative fixture: Freeze the basket to a single security: option-selection value must vanish; change conversion factor units and require invoice identity failure rather than silent rescaling. GO only with zero future-row acceptance and explicit fallback behavior.
3. **G2 — Credible baseline (BLOCKED on G1).** Implement Hold back 10% of eligible basket inventory and allocate remaining loans using current fees. with actual or clearly labeled scenario costs. Freeze target horizon, cohorts, primary metric and parameters before holdout. Pin dependency hashes and data manifests.
4. **G3 — Candidate challenge (BLOCKED on G2).** Compare Scenario dynamic programming for marginal delivery-option value to baseline, with Least-squares Monte Carlo with joint cash/futures/repo scenarios and integer delivery sizing reserved as an optional later branch. GO if >=2 annualized bps saving over fixed holdback across >=8 delivery cycles, no missed delivery and positive benefit at twice observed transaction costs. STOP if gains depend on hindsight cheapest-to-deliver identity.
5. **G4 — Capacity and shadow (BLOCKED on G3).** Verify Illustrative $250m affected deliverable inventory. Limited by futures obligation size and deliverable-lot depth; do not take a new directional basis trade merely to generate lending revenue. At least 60 eligible decisions and 90 calendar days, or longer to include a full native seasonal/event cycle, are required before production review. Rarity-driven insufficient samples remain BLOCKED; synthetic shocks cannot count as real economic observations.
6. **G5 — Production review (BLOCKED on G4).** Reconcile lendable face limits and delivery-feasibility certificate with a private independently maintained reference, confirm operational ownership and rollback, then request separate deployment/trading authority. Any hard-constraint breach halts the pilot.

Release the simplest model that passes all gates; abandon if measured break-even annual benefit from README cannot be justified. A statistically interesting negative result may still support publication: High: interaction of delivery options and lending availability; empirical claim requires licensed synchronized markets.
