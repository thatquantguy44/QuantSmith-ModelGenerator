# Research-agent contract — Treasury Futures Delivery Inventory Reservation

**Authority:** analyze United States; Treasury futures deliverable baskets and securities loans using public, rights-approved or synthetic inputs; propose experiments and auditable recommendations. This contract does not instantiate an agent executor. Do not trade, message counterparties, repricing-contracts, certify legal rights, grant data access, deploy or merge.

**Inputs:** Published conversion factors and contract rules; futures and cash bid/ask, repo fees, loan terms, delivery notices and hedge positions. Public basket tables are insufficient for option valuation. Require `CUSIP x futures contract x observation timestamp` plus source/version/hash, unit/currency and the common time envelope. Public run mode rejects permissioned adapters and vendor extracts whose redistribution has not been approved.

**Decision scope:** Reserve eligible inventory for an existing futures short versus lending it, charging the marginal delivery-option opportunity cost. Build invoice value, basket eligibility and marginal delivery option shadow cost, compare baseline `Hold back 10% of eligible basket inventory and allocate remaining loans using current fees.` with `Scenario dynamic programming for marginal delivery-option value`, and return lendable face limits and delivery-feasibility certificate. Preserve failed fits and HOLD/BLOCKED actions.

**Evidence gates:** distinguish FACT (a linked source supports precisely the stated claim), HYPOTHESIS (the new research question), ASSUMPTION (cost/capacity/timing), CALCULATION (explicit inputs/formula) and UNVERIFIED. A paper or matching citation span cannot establish a new empirical effect. No statement of outperformance is permitted before [EVALS](EVALS.md) and QMG-20261116-04-AC-01 pass.

**Hard controls:** Reserve at 15:00 America/Chicago using quotes available by 14:55 and current contract basket version; subsequent delivery notices are outcomes, never predictive features. Contract units, invoice rounding and delivery rules must be versioned and independently reconciled before optimization. Freeze the basket to a single security: option-selection value must vanish; change conversion factor units and require invoice identity failure rather than silent rescaling.

**Escalation:** missing rights, contradictory terms, unknown availability or infeasibility => BLOCKED with affected IDs and minimal reproducible evidence. Economics review uses the README ROI formula; never fill missing prices with synthetic values without explicit synthetic labeling. Human domain reviewer assesses Endogenous cheapest-to-deliver switches, delivery timing rules and fee cancellation terms matter; mark-to-model delivery value is not realized cash profit.
