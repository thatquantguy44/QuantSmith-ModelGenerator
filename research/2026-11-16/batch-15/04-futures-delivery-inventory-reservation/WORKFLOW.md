# Research workflow — Treasury Futures Delivery Inventory Reservation

1. **Data => rights/time audit.** Ingest CUSIP x futures contract x observation timestamp through proposed `treasury_delivery`; save exact release bytes privately where rights permit and only public-safe manifests here. Check Reserve at 15:00 America/Chicago using quotes available by 14:55 and current contract basket version; subsequent delivery notices are outcomes, never predictive features. Unknown data rights or availability yields BLOCKED.
2. **Hypothesis => pre-registration.** Freeze A delivery-option reserve beats a fixed percentage holdback by at least 2 bps of annualized affected inventory value, after replacement, lending and hedge costs. Define native-horizon targets and independent event groups; lock costs, limits and partition dates before seeing holdout outcomes.
3. **Baseline => reproducible control.** Implement Hold back 10% of eligible basket inventory and allocate remaining loans using current fees. Verify Freeze the basket to a single security: option-selection value must vanish; change conversion factor units and require invoice identity failure rather than silent rescaling. Keep equal information and economic constraints across methods.
4. **Candidate => interpretable action.** Build invoice value, basket eligibility and marginal delivery option shadow cost; fit/solve Scenario dynamic programming for marginal delivery-option value. Return lendable face limits and delivery-feasibility certificate plus a feasibility/time certificate. Advanced research `Least-squares Monte Carlo with joint cash/futures/repo scenarios and integer delivery sizing` stays optional.
5. **Validation => objective verdict.** Report total net economics, forced replacement, delivery feasibility, hedge error and option-value stability. GO if >=2 annualized bps saving over fixed holdback across >=8 delivery cycles, no missed delivery and positive benefit at twice observed transaction costs. STOP if gains depend on hindsight cheapest-to-deliver identity. Double execution costs and halve capacity; include zero-benefit and delayed-launch ROI scenarios.
6. **Review => private shadow.** Domain reviewer challenges Endogenous cheapest-to-deliver switches, delivery timing rules and fee cancellation terms matter; mark-to-model delivery value is not realized cash profit. Complete rights and capacity evidence before any prospective shadow run. Human review is required; runtime deployment and trading need separate authority.

All transitions are future gates, currently BLOCKED. A failed experiment is retained with source/config/code hashes; reruns cannot overwrite an adverse outcome or silently relax a threshold. Generated reports label FACT/HYPOTHESIS/ASSUMPTION/CALCULATION/UNVERIFIED and preserve the original baseline alongside candidate output.
