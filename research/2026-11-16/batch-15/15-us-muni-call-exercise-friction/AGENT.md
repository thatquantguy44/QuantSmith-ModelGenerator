# Research-agent contract — US Municipal Call-Exercise Friction Surface

**Authority:** analyze United States tax-exempt callable municipal bonds using public, rights-approved or synthetic inputs; propose experiments and auditable recommendations. This contract does not instantiate an agent executor. Do not trade, message counterparties, repricing-contracts, certify legal rights, grant data access, deploy or merge.

**Inputs:** EMMA official statements and public call notices, contemporaneously released issuer financials, trades with dissemination timestamps, call schedules, accrued interest and matched yield curves. Public browsing is not assumed licensed bulk extraction. Require `bond x call opportunity x public notice/financial filing event` plus source/version/hash, unit/currency and the common time envelope. Public run mode rejects permissioned adapters and vendor extracts whose redistribution has not been approved.

**Decision scope:** Choose between matched callable bonds using a calibrated call hazard and cash-flow stress rather than yield-to-worst alone. Build refunding incentive, notice state, issuer friction and time-to-call, compare baseline `Exercise at first call date when modeled refunding savings exceed a fixed 3% threshold; compare yield-to-worst selection.` with `Discrete-time survival model with economic refunding covariates`, and return call probability surface, cash-flow distribution and duration-matched selection report. Preserve failed fits and HOLD/BLOCKED actions.

**Evidence gates:** distinguish FACT (a linked source supports precisely the stated claim), HYPOTHESIS (the new research question), ASSUMPTION (cost/capacity/timing), CALCULATION (explicit inputs/formula) and UNVERIFIED. A paper or matching citation span cannot establish a new empirical effect. No statement of outperformance is permitted before [EVALS](EVALS.md) and QMG-20261116-15-AC-01 pass.

**Hard controls:** Use only disclosures disseminated by prior close; trade at next obtainable firm offer/bid with a one-session latency. A call notice must enter at publication, not retroactively at the optional call date. Every parsed call term needs a document page/span; conflicting extraordinary versus optional redemption clauses block valuation. Insert a public call notice after the prior-close cutoff: the model must not predict certainty before disclosure. A noncallable bond must have zero optional call hazard.

**Escalation:** missing rights, contradictory terms, unknown availability or infeasibility => BLOCKED with affected IDs and minimal reproducible evidence. Economics review uses the README ROI formula; never fill missing prices with synthetic values without explicit synthetic labeling. Human domain reviewer assesses Issuer credit, tax law, refunding restrictions and tender/redemption mechanics vary. Trade marks are not executable offers; repeated bonds from one issuer inflate sample size.
