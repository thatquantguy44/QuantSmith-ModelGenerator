# Research workflow — US Municipal Call-Exercise Friction Surface

1. **Data => rights/time audit.** Ingest bond x call opportunity x public notice/financial filing event through proposed `muni_call_disclosures`; save exact release bytes privately where rights permit and only public-safe manifests here. Check Use only disclosures disseminated by prior close; trade at next obtainable firm offer/bid with a one-session latency. A call notice must enter at publication, not retroactively at the optional call date. Unknown data rights or availability yields BLOCKED.
2. **Hypothesis => pre-registration.** Freeze Issuer-friction call probabilities reduce one-year cash-flow forecast error by at least 15% versus deterministic first-economic-call exercise and improve net matched-bond selection by 15 bps per annum. Define native-horizon targets and independent event groups; lock costs, limits and partition dates before seeing holdout outcomes.
3. **Baseline => reproducible control.** Implement Exercise at first call date when modeled refunding savings exceed a fixed 3% threshold; compare yield-to-worst selection. Verify Insert a public call notice after the prior-close cutoff: the model must not predict certainty before disclosure. A noncallable bond must have zero optional call hazard. Keep equal information and economic constraints across methods.
4. **Candidate => interpretable action.** Build refunding incentive, notice state, issuer friction and time-to-call; fit/solve Discrete-time survival model with economic refunding covariates. Return call probability surface, cash-flow distribution and duration-matched selection report plus a feasibility/time certificate. Advanced research `Hierarchical issuer stopping model with financing frictions and cash-flow scenario valuation` stays optional.
5. **Validation => objective verdict.** Report Brier score, cash-flow MAE, duration error, net matched return, spread/size sensitivity and call calibration. GO if >=15% cash-flow MAE reduction and >=15 net annualized bps on >=500 held-out call opportunities; STOP if trade-vs-quote bias or missing failed/uncalled bonds explains gains. Double execution costs and halve capacity; include zero-benefit and delayed-launch ROI scenarios.
6. **Review => private shadow.** Domain reviewer challenges Issuer credit, tax law, refunding restrictions and tender/redemption mechanics vary. Trade marks are not executable offers; repeated bonds from one issuer inflate sample size. Complete rights and capacity evidence before any prospective shadow run. Human review is required; runtime deployment and trading need separate authority.

All transitions are future gates, currently BLOCKED. A failed experiment is retained with source/config/code hashes; reruns cannot overwrite an adverse outcome or silently relax a threshold. Generated reports label FACT/HYPOTHESIS/ASSUMPTION/CALCULATION/UNVERIFIED and preserve the original baseline alongside candidate output.
