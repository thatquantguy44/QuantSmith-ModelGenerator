# Roadmap — US Municipal Call-Exercise Friction Surface

**Effort assumption:** One municipal researcher and one document/data engineer for 12-16 weeks. Elapsed time and ROI are conditional, not commitments.

1. **G0 — Evidence and rights (BLOCKED).** Resolve the ranked ladder in SOURCES. Obtain a timestamp/units sample for bond x call opportunity x public notice/financial filing event; identify incomplete public coverage. STOP if required economic targets or first-public times cannot be recovered legally.
2. **G1 — Synthetic contract proof (BLOCKED on G0).** Implement the as-of envelope, refunding incentive, notice state, issuer friction and time-to-call, and independent negative fixture: Insert a public call notice after the prior-close cutoff: the model must not predict certainty before disclosure. A noncallable bond must have zero optional call hazard. GO only with zero future-row acceptance and explicit fallback behavior.
3. **G2 — Credible baseline (BLOCKED on G1).** Implement Exercise at first call date when modeled refunding savings exceed a fixed 3% threshold; compare yield-to-worst selection. with actual or clearly labeled scenario costs. Freeze target horizon, cohorts, primary metric and parameters before holdout. Pin dependency hashes and data manifests.
4. **G3 — Candidate challenge (BLOCKED on G2).** Compare Discrete-time survival model with economic refunding covariates to baseline, with Hierarchical issuer stopping model with financing frictions and cash-flow scenario valuation reserved as an optional later branch. GO if >=15% cash-flow MAE reduction and >=15 net annualized bps on >=500 held-out call opportunities; STOP if trade-vs-quote bias or missing failed/uncalled bonds explains gains.
5. **G4 — Capacity and shadow (BLOCKED on G3).** Verify Illustrative $40m matched-bond sleeve; cap issue ownership and trade size relative to observed turnover, allow days to execute and charge dealer markup. No presumption of instantaneous municipal execution. At least 60 eligible decisions and 90 calendar days, or longer to include a full native seasonal/event cycle, are required before production review. Rarity-driven insufficient samples remain BLOCKED; synthetic shocks cannot count as real economic observations.
6. **G5 — Production review (BLOCKED on G4).** Reconcile call probability surface, cash-flow distribution and duration-matched selection report with a private independently maintained reference, confirm operational ownership and rollback, then request separate deployment/trading authority. Any hard-constraint breach halts the pilot.

Release the simplest model that passes all gates; abandon if measured break-even annual benefit from README cannot be justified. A statistically interesting negative result may still support publication: High: exercise frictions and segmented fixed income; strong public-document reproducibility potential.
