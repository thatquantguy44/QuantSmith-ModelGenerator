# Research-agent contract — Securities-Loan Dust-Lot Consolidation

**Authority:** analyze US and developed-market equity lending using public, rights-approved or synthetic inputs; propose experiments and auditable recommendations. This contract does not instantiate an agent executor. Do not trade, message counterparties, repricing-contracts, certify legal rights, grant data access, deploy or merge.

**Inputs:** Synthetic owner/borrower inventories, approved minimum lots, source shares, per-ticket servicing fees, loan quantities, movement cost and owner allocation rules. No real owner or borrower data are committed. Require `owner x borrower x security loan lot x service day` plus source/version/hash, unit/currency and the common time envelope. Public run mode rejects permissioned adapters and vendor extracts whose redistribution has not been approved.

**Decision scope:** Consolidate or close eligible small lots within the same owner/borrower/legal permissions while preserving economic exposure and service constraints. Build fixed servicing charge, permitted owner-lot map and movement-cost matrix, compare baseline `Pro-rata eligible allocation across owners, subject to existing concentration caps.` with `Fixed-charge transportation MILP`, and return consolidation suggestions with per-owner exposure/revenue reconciliation. Preserve failed fits and HOLD/BLOCKED actions.

**Evidence gates:** distinguish FACT (a linked source supports precisely the stated claim), HYPOTHESIS (the new research question), ASSUMPTION (cost/capacity/timing), CALCULATION (explicit inputs/formula) and UNVERIFIED. A paper or matching citation span cannot establish a new empirical effect. No statement of outperformance is permitted before [EVALS](EVALS.md) and QMG-20261116-10-AC-01 pass.

**Hard controls:** Solve after confirmed end-of-day books and before next-day instruction cutoff; only booked demand and valid consent known at solve time enter. Settled movements are later outcomes. Deterministic tie-breaking and original-book fallback on infeasibility; quantity and owner revenue reconcile exactly to declared permitted changes. Set per-ticket cost to zero: consolidation must not invent savings; introduce an owner-specific nontransferability flag and verify its lot remains untouched.

**Escalation:** missing rights, contradictory terms, unknown availability or infeasibility => BLOCKED with affected IDs and minimal reproducible evidence. Economics review uses the README ROI formula; never fill missing prices with synthetic values without explicit synthetic labeling. Human domain reviewer assesses Tax lots, revenue attribution, beneficial-owner fairness and contract permissions can prohibit economically appealing consolidation. Unit-cost assumptions often dominate the result.
