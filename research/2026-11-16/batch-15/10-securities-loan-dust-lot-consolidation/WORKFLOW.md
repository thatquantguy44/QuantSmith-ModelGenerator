# Research workflow — Securities-Loan Dust-Lot Consolidation

1. **Data => rights/time audit.** Ingest owner x borrower x security loan lot x service day through proposed `loan_lots`; save exact release bytes privately where rights permit and only public-safe manifests here. Check Solve after confirmed end-of-day books and before next-day instruction cutoff; only booked demand and valid consent known at solve time enter. Settled movements are later outcomes. Unknown data rights or availability yields BLOCKED.
2. **Hypothesis => pre-registration.** Freeze Integer cost-aware lot consolidation cuts recurring servicing expense by at least 20% relative to pro-rata allocation without reducing net lending revenue by more than 1%. Define native-horizon targets and independent event groups; lock costs, limits and partition dates before seeing holdout outcomes.
3. **Baseline => reproducible control.** Implement Pro-rata eligible allocation across owners, subject to existing concentration caps. Verify Set per-ticket cost to zero: consolidation must not invent savings; introduce an owner-specific nontransferability flag and verify its lot remains untouched. Keep equal information and economic constraints across methods.
4. **Candidate => interpretable action.** Build fixed servicing charge, permitted owner-lot map and movement-cost matrix; fit/solve Fixed-charge transportation MILP. Return consolidation suggestions with per-owner exposure/revenue reconciliation plus a feasibility/time certificate. Advanced research `Multi-period lot consolidation with persistence penalties and uncertain demand bands` stays optional.
5. **Validation => objective verdict.** Report servicing cost, active lots, net revenue, owner fairness constraints, turnover and time to solve. GO if >=20% servicing-cost saving and <=1% net revenue loss, zero owner-rights violations and payback within 12 months on measured cost inputs. STOP if gains disappear after movement fees or permitted allocations are fixed. Double execution costs and halve capacity; include zero-benefit and delayed-launch ROI scenarios.
6. **Review => private shadow.** Domain reviewer challenges Tax lots, revenue attribution, beneficial-owner fairness and contract permissions can prohibit economically appealing consolidation. Unit-cost assumptions often dominate the result. Complete rights and capacity evidence before any prospective shadow run. Human review is required; runtime deployment and trading need separate authority.

All transitions are future gates, currently BLOCKED. A failed experiment is retained with source/config/code hashes; reruns cannot overwrite an adverse outcome or silently relax a threshold. Generated reports label FACT/HYPOTHESIS/ASSUMPTION/CALCULATION/UNVERIFIED and preserve the original baseline alongside candidate output.
