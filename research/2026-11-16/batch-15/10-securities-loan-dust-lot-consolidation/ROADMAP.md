# Roadmap — Securities-Loan Dust-Lot Consolidation

**Effort assumption:** One optimization engineer and part-time lending researcher for 6-8 weeks. Elapsed time and ROI are conditional, not commitments.

1. **G0 — Evidence and rights (BLOCKED).** Resolve the ranked ladder in SOURCES. Obtain a timestamp/units sample for owner x borrower x security loan lot x service day; identify incomplete public coverage. STOP if required economic targets or first-public times cannot be recovered legally.
2. **G1 — Synthetic contract proof (BLOCKED on G0).** Implement the as-of envelope, fixed servicing charge, permitted owner-lot map and movement-cost matrix, and independent negative fixture: Set per-ticket cost to zero: consolidation must not invent savings; introduce an owner-specific nontransferability flag and verify its lot remains untouched. GO only with zero future-row acceptance and explicit fallback behavior.
3. **G2 — Credible baseline (BLOCKED on G1).** Implement Pro-rata eligible allocation across owners, subject to existing concentration caps. with actual or clearly labeled scenario costs. Freeze target horizon, cohorts, primary metric and parameters before holdout. Pin dependency hashes and data manifests.
4. **G3 — Candidate challenge (BLOCKED on G2).** Compare Fixed-charge transportation MILP to baseline, with Multi-period lot consolidation with persistence penalties and uncertain demand bands reserved as an optional later branch. GO if >=20% servicing-cost saving and <=1% net revenue loss, zero owner-rights violations and payback within 12 months on measured cost inputs. STOP if gains disappear after movement fees or permitted allocations are fixed.
5. **G4 — Capacity and shadow (BLOCKED on G3).** Verify Illustrative 50,000 serviced lots per year; no market ADV capacity claim. Benefits limited by actual removable lots and movement/consent cost, not gross notional. At least 60 eligible decisions and 90 calendar days, or longer to include a full native seasonal/event cycle, are required before production review. Rarity-driven insufficient samples remain BLOCKED; synthetic shocks cannot count as real economic observations.
6. **G5 — Production review (BLOCKED on G4).** Reconcile consolidation suggestions with per-owner exposure/revenue reconciliation with a private independently maintained reference, confirm operational ownership and rollback, then request separate deployment/trading authority. Any hard-constraint breach halts the pilot.

Release the simplest model that passes all gates; abandon if measured break-even annual benefit from README cannot be justified. A statistically interesting negative result may still support publication: Medium: fixed-charge inventory allocation with fair attribution; production engineering likely more valuable than model novelty.
