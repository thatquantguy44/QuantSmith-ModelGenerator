# Research workflow — India Bond Index-Flow Saturation

1. **Data => rights/time audit.** Ingest government bond x announcement/rebalance cohort x public flow release through proposed `india_index_events`; save exact release bytes privately where rights permit and only public-safe manifests here. Check Act next Mumbai session after a verified inclusion announcement or ownership release; close at a predeclared post-effective window. Never use final index weights or future ownership before announcement. Unknown data rights or availability yields BLOCKED.
2. **Hypothesis => pre-registration.** Freeze Expected demand scaled by free-float turnover predicts inclusion-window residual returns better than an inclusion dummy, and improves net basket return by at least 20 bps per annum. Define native-horizon targets and independent event groups; lock costs, limits and partition dates before seeing holdout outcomes.
3. **Baseline => reproducible control.** Implement Equal-risk inclusion-event basket versus duration-matched eligible controls. Verify Assign the same event to noneligible placebo bonds and to dates six months earlier; no persistent significant effect should survive cohort-level multiple-testing adjustment. Keep equal information and economic constraints across methods.
4. **Candidate => interpretable action.** Build announced flow pressure, free-float turnover and lagged foreign ownership saturation; fit/solve Matched staggered event study with demand-pressure interactions. Return event-study estimands, placebo diagnostics and duration-neutral research basket plus a feasibility/time certificate. Advanced research `Doubly robust event design plus a structural price-impact saturation curve` stays optional.
5. **Validation => objective verdict.** Report net residual bps, pretrend diagnostics, placebo effects, DV01 neutrality, turnover and sensitivity to AUM bounds. GO if >=20 net annualized bps improvement and event-cluster confidence lower bound >0; pretrends must pass a specified +/-10 bp equivalence band. STOP if fewer than 3 independent cohorts or ownership/AUM histories are reconstructed retrospectively. Double execution costs and halve capacity; include zero-benefit and delayed-launch ROI scenarios.
6. **Review => private shadow.** Domain reviewer challenges Eligibility correlates with liquidity and issuance; parallel trends may fail. Single historical inclusion episodes cannot support strong causal or scalable trading claims. Complete rights and capacity evidence before any prospective shadow run. Human review is required; runtime deployment and trading need separate authority.

All transitions are future gates, currently BLOCKED. A failed experiment is retained with source/config/code hashes; reruns cannot overwrite an adverse outcome or silently relax a threshold. Generated reports label FACT/HYPOTHESIS/ASSUMPTION/CALCULATION/UNVERIFIED and preserve the original baseline alongside candidate output.
