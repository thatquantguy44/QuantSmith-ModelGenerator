# Research-agent contract — India Bond Index-Flow Saturation

**Authority:** analyze India government bonds; emerging-market capital flows using public, rights-approved or synthetic inputs; propose experiments and auditable recommendations. This contract does not instantiate an agent executor. Do not trade, message counterparties, repricing-contracts, certify legal rights, grant data access, deploy or merge.

**Inputs:** RBI FAR/public market context, dated index-provider inclusion/weight notices, foreign ownership/flows, bond eligibility, free-float and issue cash flows; local executable price and hedge history. Require `government bond x announcement/rebalance cohort x public flow release` plus source/version/hash, unit/currency and the common time envelope. Public run mode rejects permissioned adapters and vendor extracts whose redistribution has not been approved.

**Decision scope:** Tilt duration-neutral eligible-versus-near-eligible government bond baskets using dated projected passive demand and realized foreign-flow saturation. Build announced flow pressure, free-float turnover and lagged foreign ownership saturation, compare baseline `Equal-risk inclusion-event basket versus duration-matched eligible controls.` with `Matched staggered event study with demand-pressure interactions`, and return event-study estimands, placebo diagnostics and duration-neutral research basket. Preserve failed fits and HOLD/BLOCKED actions.

**Evidence gates:** distinguish FACT (a linked source supports precisely the stated claim), HYPOTHESIS (the new research question), ASSUMPTION (cost/capacity/timing), CALCULATION (explicit inputs/formula) and UNVERIFIED. A paper or matching citation span cannot establish a new empirical effect. No statement of outperformance is permitted before [EVALS](EVALS.md) and QMG-20261116-14-AC-01 pass.

**Hard controls:** Act next Mumbai session after a verified inclusion announcement or ownership release; close at a predeclared post-effective window. Never use final index weights or future ownership before announcement. Announced and effective membership must occupy separate columns; unknown original announcement time blocks that cohort. Assign the same event to noneligible placebo bonds and to dates six months earlier; no persistent significant effect should survive cohort-level multiple-testing adjustment.

**Escalation:** missing rights, contradictory terms, unknown availability or infeasibility => BLOCKED with affected IDs and minimal reproducible evidence. Economics review uses the README ROI formula; never fill missing prices with synthetic values without explicit synthetic labeling. Human domain reviewer assesses Eligibility correlates with liquidity and issuance; parallel trends may fail. Single historical inclusion episodes cannot support strong causal or scalable trading claims.
