1. **Data => rights/time audit.** Ingest repo ticket and dated cash/security obligation through proposed `repo_ledger`; save exact release bytes privately where rights permit and only public-safe manifests here. Check Freeze the ledger at 14:00 America/New_York; use only confirmed records ingested by 13:55 and decisions before the relevant service cutoff. Cash-flow amendments received later enter the next solve. Unknown data rights or availability yields BLOCKED.
2. **Hypothesis => pre-registration.** Freeze An economics-preserving compression reduces eligible gross ticket notional by at least 15% and operational cost by at least 10% versus bilateral pair cancellation, with no additional settlement or market risk. Define native-horizon targets and independent event groups; lock costs, limits and partition dates before seeing holdout outcomes.
3. **Baseline => reproducible control.** Implement Cancel exact opposite tickets bilaterally within a legal set; otherwise hold the original book. Verify Construct a three-node cycle with mismatched maturity dates: it must remain uncompressed; changing one consent flag must remove every affected action. Keep equal information and economic constraints across methods.
4. **Candidate => interpretable action.** Build dated incidence matrices, lot constraints and consent mask; fit/solve Mixed-integer minimum-cost circulation with exact cash-flow conservation. Return proposed tear-up/resizing schedule, exact flow certificate and rejected-action reasons plus a feasibility/time certificate. Advanced research `Robust multi-period compression with consent uncertainty and settlement-feasibility cuts` stays optional.
5. **Validation => objective verdict.** Report gross reduction, message reduction, all-in cost, maximum flow residual, consent acceptance and solve time. GO only with >=15% gross reduction and >=10% realized operational cost reduction on held-out eligible sets; every cash/security residual <=$0.01 cash or zero security units; zero consent violations. STOP on any economic transfer or if gains vanish after legal filtering. Double execution costs and halve capacity; include zero-benefit and delayed-launch ROI scenarios.
6. **Review => private shadow.** Domain reviewer challenges Legal netting is not created by algebra; different dates, rates and counterparties must not be silently offset. Synthetic network density can overstate capacity. Complete rights and capacity evidence before any prospective shadow run. Human review is required; runtime deployment and trading need separate authority.

All transitions are future gates, currently BLOCKED. A failed experiment is retained with source/config/code hashes; reruns cannot overwrite an adverse outcome or silently relax a threshold. Generated reports label FACT/HYPOTHESIS/ASSUMPTION/CALCULATION/UNVERIFIED and preserve the original baseline alongside candidate output.
