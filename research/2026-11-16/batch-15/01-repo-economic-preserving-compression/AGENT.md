# Research-agent contract — Repo Compression with Cash-Flow Preservation

**Authority:** analyze US Treasury repo; one legal netting set at a time using public, rights-approved or synthetic inputs; propose experiments and auditable recommendations. This contract does not instantiate an agent executor. Do not trade, message counterparties, repricing-contracts, certify legal rights, grant data access, deploy or merge.

**Inputs:** Ticket ID, legal-set ID, participant pseudonym, CUSIP, start/end timestamps, cash principal, accrued interest, rate convention, face amount, consent flag and minimum lot. Public rules plus synthetic networks first; public aggregates cannot reconstruct a private trade graph. Require `repo ticket and dated cash/security obligation` plus source/version/hash, unit/currency and the common time envelope. Public run mode rejects permissioned adapters and vendor extracts whose redistribution has not been approved.

**Decision scope:** Select existing tickets to tear up or resize without changing each participant's dated cash and CUSIP obligations. Build dated incidence matrices, lot constraints and consent mask, compare baseline `Cancel exact opposite tickets bilaterally within a legal set; otherwise hold the original book.` with `Mixed-integer minimum-cost circulation with exact cash-flow conservation`, and return proposed tear-up/resizing schedule, exact flow certificate and rejected-action reasons. Preserve failed fits and HOLD/BLOCKED actions.

**Evidence gates:** distinguish FACT (a linked source supports precisely the stated claim), HYPOTHESIS (the new research question), ASSUMPTION (cost/capacity/timing), CALCULATION (explicit inputs/formula) and UNVERIFIED. A paper or matching citation span cannot establish a new empirical effect. No statement of outperformance is permitted before [EVALS](EVALS.md) and QMG-20261116-01-AC-01 pass.

**Hard controls:** Freeze the ledger at 14:00 America/New_York; use only confirmed records ingested by 13:55 and decisions before the relevant service cutoff. Cash-flow amendments received later enter the next solve. For a proposed 5,000-ticket set, target p95 solve time <=60 seconds and exact conservation certificate; infeasible or timed-out solves return original book. Construct a three-node cycle with mismatched maturity dates: it must remain uncompressed; changing one consent flag must remove every affected action.

**Escalation:** missing rights, contradictory terms, unknown availability or infeasibility => BLOCKED with affected IDs and minimal reproducible evidence. Economics review uses the README ROI formula; never fill missing prices with synthetic values without explicit synthetic labeling. Human domain reviewer assesses Legal netting is not created by algebra; different dates, rates and counterparties must not be silently offset. Synthetic network density can overstate capacity.
