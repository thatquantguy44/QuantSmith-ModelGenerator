# Research-agent contract — Repo Benchmark Outage and Revision Risk Monitor

**Authority:** analyze United States overnight secured funding using public, rights-approved or synthetic inputs; propose experiments and auditable recommendations. This contract does not instantiate an agent executor. Do not trade, message counterparties, repricing-contracts, certify legal rights, grant data access, deploy or merge.

**Inputs:** Official rate releases, first-observed timestamps, revision footnotes, contingency notices, rates and ranges; OFR lagged aggregates may inform monitoring but are not contract benchmark substitutes. Require `reference rate x publication event x revision version` plus source/version/hash, unit/currency and the common time envelope. Public run mode rejects permissioned adapters and vendor extracts whose redistribution has not been approved.

**Decision scope:** Choose whether a funding-risk report may use a published rate, show an uncertainty interval, or require manual review; never replace a contractual fallback. Build rate release age, version chain, contingency flag and interval width, compare baseline `Carry the most recent official rate and mark missing releases only after end of day.` with `Versioned publication-state rules plus robust short-horizon rate interval`, and return accept/interval/review status, immutable as-of report and official-source pointer. Preserve failed fits and HOLD/BLOCKED actions.

**Evidence gates:** distinguish FACT (a linked source supports precisely the stated claim), HYPOTHESIS (the new research question), ASSUMPTION (cost/capacity/timing), CALCULATION (explicit inputs/formula) and UNVERIFIED. A paper or matching citation span cannot establish a new empirical effect. No statement of outperformance is permitted before [EVALS](EVALS.md) and QMG-20261116-09-AC-01 pass.

**Hard controls:** Snapshot at 08:15 America/New_York and again after verified revisions; only information received by each timestamp is usable. A 14:30 revision must not overwrite the morning feature snapshot. Append-only publication ledger; fail closed on missing source/version and keep synthetic monitoring estimates in a separate schema. Hide a morning release and reveal a later correction: morning output must stay immutable and explicitly uncertain; no synthetic estimate may populate a contractual-rate field.

**Escalation:** missing rights, contradictory terms, unknown availability or infeasibility => BLOCKED with affected IDs and minimal reproducible evidence. Economics review uses the README ROI formula; never fill missing prices with synthetic values without explicit synthetic labeling. Human domain reviewer assesses Contingency/revision events are rare; injected outages are software stress tests, not evidence of historical economic benefit. OFR mean rates and official medians differ in coverage.
