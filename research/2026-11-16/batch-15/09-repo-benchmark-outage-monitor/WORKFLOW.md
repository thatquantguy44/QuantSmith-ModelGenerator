# Research workflow — Repo Benchmark Outage and Revision Risk Monitor

1. **Data => rights/time audit.** Ingest reference rate x publication event x revision version through proposed `repo_publication_events`; save exact release bytes privately where rights permit and only public-safe manifests here. Check Snapshot at 08:15 America/New_York and again after verified revisions; only information received by each timestamp is usable. A 14:30 revision must not overwrite the morning feature snapshot. Unknown data rights or availability yields BLOCKED.
2. **Hypothesis => pre-registration.** Freeze A publication-state monitor lowers material funding-risk reporting errors by at least 50% versus blindly carrying the last rate, with false escalation on fewer than 2% of ordinary releases. Define native-horizon targets and independent event groups; lock costs, limits and partition dates before seeing holdout outcomes.
3. **Baseline => reproducible control.** Implement Carry the most recent official rate and mark missing releases only after end of day. Verify Hide a morning release and reveal a later correction: morning output must stay immutable and explicitly uncertain; no synthetic estimate may populate a contractual-rate field. Keep equal information and economic constraints across methods.
4. **Candidate => interpretable action.** Build rate release age, version chain, contingency flag and interval width; fit/solve Versioned publication-state rules plus robust short-horizon rate interval. Return accept/interval/review status, immutable as-of report and official-source pointer plus a feasibility/time certificate. Advanced research `Regime-switching Bayesian filtering with missing-source stress and calibrated selective reporting` stays optional.
5. **Validation => objective verdict.** Report basis-point error, dollar sensitivity error, interval coverage, false escalations, detection latency and audit completeness. GO if >=50% reduction in material (>2 bp monitoring error) incidents and <2% false escalation over >=250 releases plus synthetic outages; interval coverage >=99%. STOP if rare-event evidence cannot separate improvement from excessive review. Double execution costs and halve capacity; include zero-benefit and delayed-launch ROI scenarios.
6. **Review => private shadow.** Domain reviewer challenges Contingency/revision events are rare; injected outages are software stress tests, not evidence of historical economic benefit. OFR mean rates and official medians differ in coverage. Complete rights and capacity evidence before any prospective shadow run. Human review is required; runtime deployment and trading need separate authority.

All transitions are future gates, currently BLOCKED. A failed experiment is retained with source/config/code hashes; reruns cannot overwrite an adverse outcome or silently relax a threshold. Generated reports label FACT/HYPOTHESIS/ASSUMPTION/CALCULATION/UNVERIFIED and preserve the original baseline alongside candidate output.
