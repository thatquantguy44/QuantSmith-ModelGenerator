# Research-agent contract — Japan Buyback Completion Credibility

**Authority:** analyze Japan listed equities; developed-market event research using public, rights-approved or synthetic inputs; propose experiments and auditable recommendations. This contract does not instantiate an agent executor. Do not trade, message counterparties, repricing-contracts, certify legal rights, grant data access, deploy or merge.

**Inputs:** Public issuer/EDINET buyback announcements and monthly completion filings, JPX procedures, cancellation/reissue terms, sector/free-float history, delisted returns and licensed executable prices. J-Quants Pro is a licensed convenience, not assumed free bulk access. Require `issuer x announced buyback program x filing acceptance event` plus source/version/hash, unit/currency and the common time envelope. Public run mode rejects permissioned adapters and vendor extracts whose redistribution has not been approved.

**Decision scope:** Weight an announcement-event equity sleeve by ex-ante completion probability rather than headline authorized buyback size. Build prior completion rate, cash-funded capacity, expiry horizon and free-float-normalized authorization, compare baseline `Authorization divided by contemporaneously known float market cap, with the same neutral portfolio and execution rules.` with `Calibrated discrete-time completion hazard with constrained event portfolio`, and return completion probabilities and neutral event-sleeve research orders. Preserve failed fits and HOLD/BLOCKED actions.

**Evidence gates:** distinguish FACT (a linked source supports precisely the stated claim), HYPOTHESIS (the new research question), ASSUMPTION (cost/capacity/timing), CALCULATION (explicit inputs/formula) and UNVERIFIED. A paper or matching citation span cannot establish a new empirical effect. No statement of outperformance is permitted before [EVALS](EVALS.md) and QMG-20261116-11-AC-01 pass.

**Hard controls:** Enter at next Tokyo session VWAP after a public announcement plus 30 minutes processing; filings arriving after the next-open cutoff shift another session. Completion reports update only when publicly filed. Retain Japanese original text, accession ID and extraction span; any translation/model extraction without supporting span is excluded. Shift completion filings to their reporting-period end: leakage checker must reject the join; include terminated programs and delisted issuers with zero future authorization assumed.

**Escalation:** missing rights, contradictory terms, unknown availability or infeasibility => BLOCKED with affected IDs and minimal reproducible evidence. Economics review uses the README ROI formula; never fill missing prices with synthetic values without explicit synthetic labeling. Human domain reviewer assesses Completion is endogenous to stock prices, governance changes and earnings; predictive results do not establish buyback causation. Current issuer lists produce survivorship bias.
