# Research-agent contract — Cross-Border Settlement Calendar Bridge Planner

**Authority:** analyze US, euro area and UK financing settlement using public, rights-approved or synthetic inputs; propose experiments and auditable recommendations. This contract does not instantiate an agent executor. Do not trade, message counterparties, repricing-contracts, certify legal rights, grant data access, deploy or merge.

**Inputs:** Official service calendars and versioned cutoffs, announced closures/extensions, obligation currency/security legs and value dates, available prefunding routes and firm rates. Require `obligation leg x settlement service x UTC instant` plus source/version/hash, unit/currency and the common time envelope. Public run mode rejects permissioned adapters and vendor extracts whose redistribution has not been approved.

**Decision scope:** Choose an earlier settlement date and minimal bridge cash for already approved cross-border obligations. Build service open intervals, version effective dates, cash-day gaps and DST conversion, compare baseline `Prefund every cross-border obligation two generic business days early, with manual holiday review.` with `Time-expanded multi-service cash-flow network`, and return prefunding dates, minimal cash bridge and infeasible-leg alerts. Preserve failed fits and HOLD/BLOCKED actions.

**Evidence gates:** distinguish FACT (a linked source supports precisely the stated claim), HYPOTHESIS (the new research question), ASSUMPTION (cost/capacity/timing), CALCULATION (explicit inputs/formula) and UNVERIFIED. A paper or matching citation span cannot establish a new empirical effect. No statement of outperformance is permitted before [EVALS](EVALS.md) and QMG-20261116-08-AC-01 pass.

**Hard controls:** Plan at least two local business days before a known holiday, using rules announced by then. Unexpected outages are separate stress scenarios; announced future changes enter only after their publication. Timezone-aware UTC storage with local service timezone preserved; unknown calendar version returns BLOCKED, never a guessed weekday. Replay US/Europe DST mismatch, Good Friday and a service-specific cutoff extension; the planner must distinguish securities and cash service availability.

**Escalation:** missing rights, contradictory terms, unknown availability or infeasibility => BLOCKED with affected IDs and minimal reproducible evidence. Economics review uses the README ROI formula; never fill missing prices with synthetic values without explicit synthetic labeling. Human domain reviewer assesses Future operating-day changes differ between funds and securities services. DST, half-days and local date conventions can introduce 24-hour errors; a public calendar is not a service entitlement.
