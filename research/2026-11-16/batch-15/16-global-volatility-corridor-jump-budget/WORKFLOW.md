2. **Hypothesis => pre-registration.** Freeze A corridor/jump-aware allocation reduces 99% tail loss by at least 15% at equal net carry versus equal-vega short-variance with the same tail-hedge budget. 4. **Candidate => interpretable action.** Build corridor variance, overnight jump proxies, executable strip costs and tail hedge Greeks; fit/solve Semiparametric continuous/jump decomposition and constrained risk-budget allocation. Return vega allocation, explicit option-strip positions and stress funding requirements plus a feasibility/time certificate. 5. **Validation => objective verdict.** Report net carry, 99% ES, stress gaps, margin liquidity, hedge slippage, vega and Greeks. GO if >=15% ES reduction with mean net carry within 5% of baseline across >=8 years including held-out stress; STOP if benefit fails under doubled option spreads or cannot be implemented by listed strips. Double execution costs and halve capacity; include zero-benefit and delayed-launch ROI scenarios.
6. **Review => private shadow.** Domain reviewer challenges Sparse extreme events, stale deep-OTM quotes, mid-price replication and model-selected sampling can manufacture carry. Margin calls and holiday mismatches can dominate option premium. Complete rights and capacity evidence before any prospective shadow run. Human review is required; runtime deployment and trading need separate authority.

All transitions are future gates, currently BLOCKED. A failed experiment is retained with source/config/code hashes; reruns cannot overwrite an adverse outcome or silently relax a threshold. Generated reports label FACT/HYPOTHESIS/ASSUMPTION/CALCULATION/UNVERIFIED and preserve the original baseline alongside candidate output.
