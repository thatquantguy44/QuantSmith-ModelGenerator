# Research-agent contract — US–Europe Corridor Variance and Jump-Risk Budget

**Authority:** analyze US S&P 500 and euro-area equity index options using public, rights-approved or synthetic inputs; propose experiments and auditable recommendations. This contract does not instantiate an agent executor. Do not trade, message counterparties, repricing-contracts, certify legal rights, grant data access, deploy or merge.

**Inputs:** Public index methodology for background, synchronized bid/ask option chains, index/futures returns, expiries, dividends, rates and settlement rules. Intraday sampling and overnight jump handling are fixed before testing. Require `market x option expiry/strike x quote instant plus intraday underlying bars` plus source/version/hash, unit/currency and the common time envelope. Public run mode rejects permissioned adapters and vendor extracts whose redistribution has not been approved.

**Decision scope:** Allocate a limited short-corridor-variance risk budget across US and European indices while purchasing a defined tail hedge. Build corridor variance, overnight jump proxies, executable strip costs and tail hedge Greeks, compare baseline `Equal-vega option-strip variance exposure by market with a fixed put-spread tail hedge and identical costs.` with `Semiparametric continuous/jump decomposition and constrained risk-budget allocation`, and return vega allocation, explicit option-strip positions and stress funding requirements. Preserve failed fits and HOLD/BLOCKED actions.

**Evidence gates:** distinguish FACT (a linked source supports precisely the stated claim), HYPOTHESIS (the new research question), ASSUMPTION (cost/capacity/timing), CALCULATION (explicit inputs/formula) and UNVERIFIED. A paper or matching citation span cannot establish a new empirical effect. No statement of outperformance is permitted before [EVALS](EVALS.md) and QMG-20261116-16-AC-01 pass.

**Hard controls:** Allocate at a common UTC snapshot when both markets have liquid executable quotes; use prior completed return bars only. Do not pair a European close with later US data as contemporaneous. Reject crossed/stale quotes and impossible option monotonicity; no interpolation may create executable liquidity that was not quoted. Insert a large overnight gap with no intraday prints: loss and jump exposure must remain visible; an apparent free option caused by zero/stale bid must be rejected.

**Escalation:** missing rights, contradictory terms, unknown availability or infeasibility => BLOCKED with affected IDs and minimal reproducible evidence. Economics review uses the README ROI formula; never fill missing prices with synthetic values without explicit synthetic labeling. Human domain reviewer assesses Sparse extreme events, stale deep-OTM quotes, mid-price replication and model-selected sampling can manufacture carry. Margin calls and holiday mismatches can dominate option premium.
