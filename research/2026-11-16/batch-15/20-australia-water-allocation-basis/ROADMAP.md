# Roadmap — Australian Water Allocation Shadow Price and Crop Basis

**Effort assumption:** One agricultural researcher, water-domain reviewer and engineer for 12-18 weeks. Elapsed time and ROI are conditional, not commitments.

1. **G0 — Evidence and rights (BLOCKED).** Resolve the ranked ladder in SOURCES. Obtain a timestamp/units sample for water zone x allocation release x reported trade and crop season; identify incomplete public coverage. STOP if required economic targets or first-public times cannot be recovered legally.
2. **G1 — Synthetic contract proof (BLOCKED on G0).** Implement the as-of envelope, announced allocation innovation, transfer-zone shadow price and crop-water budget, and independent negative fixture: Give identical aggregate water to two disconnected zones: optimization must retain different shadow prices; delaying trade reporting must move its first eligible feature date. GO only with zero future-row acceptance and explicit fallback behavior.
3. **G2 — Credible baseline (BLOCKED on G1).** Implement Seasonal allocation-volume score with a fixed regional crop basket and constant futures hedge ratio. with actual or clearly labeled scenario costs. Freeze target horizon, cohorts, primary metric and parameters before holdout. Pin dependency hashes and data manifests.
4. **G3 — Candidate challenge (BLOCKED on G2).** Compare Constrained crop-water optimization with allocation-shock local projections to baseline, with Stochastic water/crop dynamic program and robust partial hedge across transfer zones reserved as an optional later branch. GO if margin MAE improves >=10% and net hedge downside loss >=5% across >=8 independently held-out seasons/zones; STOP if publication lag eliminates signal or local scarcity has no stable tradable crop exposure.
5. **G4 — Capacity and shadow (BLOCKED on G3).** Verify Illustrative $15m agricultural exposure, small research capacity. Limit futures to <=1% lagged volume; water transactions require separate legal/access review and are not assumed executable by the researcher. At least 60 eligible decisions and 90 calendar days, or longer to include a full native seasonal/event cycle, are required before production review. Rarity-driven insufficient samples remain BLOCKED; synthetic shocks cannot count as real economic observations.
6. **G5 — Production review (BLOCKED on G4).** Reconcile crop-margin scenario, local water shadow price and hedge-basis feasibility verdict with a private independently maintained reference, confirm operational ownership and rollback, then request separate deployment/trading authority. Any hard-constraint breach halts the pilot.

Release the simplest model that passes all gates; abandon if measured break-even annual benefit from README cannot be justified. A statistically interesting negative result may still support publication: High interdisciplinary potential, but weak tradable hedge linkage makes this an intentionally lower-priority contrarian project.
