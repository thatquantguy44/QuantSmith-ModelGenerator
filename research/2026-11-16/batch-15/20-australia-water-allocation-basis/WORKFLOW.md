# Research workflow — Australian Water Allocation Shadow Price and Crop Basis

1. **Data => rights/time audit.** Ingest water zone x allocation release x reported trade and crop season through proposed `australia_water_market`; save exact release bytes privately where rights permit and only public-safe manifests here. Check Update only after verified allocation announcement; water transaction price enters at public reporting time, not settlement/trade date. Trade a hedge no earlier than next liquid session; use seasons as independent split units. Unknown data rights or availability yields BLOCKED.
2. **Hypothesis => pre-registration.** Freeze Allocation-decision surprises and transfer-constrained water shadow prices improve regional crop-margin forecasts by at least 10% versus seasonal water availability, and reduce hedge downside error by 5% net of costs. Define native-horizon targets and independent event groups; lock costs, limits and partition dates before seeing holdout outcomes.
3. **Baseline => reproducible control.** Implement Seasonal allocation-volume score with a fixed regional crop basket and constant futures hedge ratio. Verify Give identical aggregate water to two disconnected zones: optimization must retain different shadow prices; delaying trade reporting must move its first eligible feature date. Keep equal information and economic constraints across methods.
4. **Candidate => interpretable action.** Build announced allocation innovation, transfer-zone shadow price and crop-water budget; fit/solve Constrained crop-water optimization with allocation-shock local projections. Return crop-margin scenario, local water shadow price and hedge-basis feasibility verdict plus a feasibility/time certificate. Advanced research `Stochastic water/crop dynamic program and robust partial hedge across transfer zones` stays optional.
5. **Validation => objective verdict.** Report crop-margin MAE, net hedge downside loss, basis risk, transfer-feasibility, water-price interval coverage and turnover. GO if margin MAE improves >=10% and net hedge downside loss >=5% across >=8 independently held-out seasons/zones; STOP if publication lag eliminates signal or local scarcity has no stable tradable crop exposure. Double execution costs and halve capacity; include zero-benefit and delayed-launch ROI scenarios.
6. **Review => private shadow.** Domain reviewer challenges Water rights, allocation trades and permanent entitlements are different assets. Thin reported prices, changing transfer limits and endogenous crop choice can defeat identification; crop futures may be a poor regional hedge. Complete rights and capacity evidence before any prospective shadow run. Human review is required; runtime deployment and trading need separate authority.

All transitions are future gates, currently BLOCKED. A failed experiment is retained with source/config/code hashes; reruns cannot overwrite an adverse outcome or silently relax a threshold. Generated reports label FACT/HYPOTHESIS/ASSUMPTION/CALCULATION/UNVERIFIED and preserve the original baseline alongside candidate output.
