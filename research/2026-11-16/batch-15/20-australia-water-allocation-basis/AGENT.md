# Research-agent contract — Australian Water Allocation Shadow Price and Crop Basis

**Authority:** analyze Murray-Darling Basin, Australia; water allocations and agricultural exposure using public, rights-approved or synthetic inputs; propose experiments and auditable recommendations. This contract does not instantiate an agent executor. Do not trade, message counterparties, repricing-contracts, certify legal rights, grant data access, deploy or merge.

**Inputs:** BOM public allocation decisions and water trades, first-publication/registration timestamps, transfer-zone limits, crop budgets, planting calendars and regional prices; licensed crop futures/basis and official agronomic response data to be resolved. Require `water zone x allocation release x reported trade and crop season` plus source/version/hash, unit/currency and the common time envelope. Public run mode rejects permissioned adapters and vendor extracts whose redistribution has not been approved.

**Decision scope:** Choose regional agricultural exposure or a crop-futures hedge only when released water scarcity changes affect a documented crop budget and survive basis costs. Build announced allocation innovation, transfer-zone shadow price and crop-water budget, compare baseline `Seasonal allocation-volume score with a fixed regional crop basket and constant futures hedge ratio.` with `Constrained crop-water optimization with allocation-shock local projections`, and return crop-margin scenario, local water shadow price and hedge-basis feasibility verdict. Preserve failed fits and HOLD/BLOCKED actions.

**Evidence gates:** distinguish FACT (a linked source supports precisely the stated claim), HYPOTHESIS (the new research question), ASSUMPTION (cost/capacity/timing), CALCULATION (explicit inputs/formula) and UNVERIFIED. A paper or matching citation span cannot establish a new empirical effect. No statement of outperformance is permitted before [EVALS](EVALS.md) and QMG-20261116-20-AC-01 pass.

**Hard controls:** Update only after verified allocation announcement; water transaction price enters at public reporting time, not settlement/trade date. Trade a hedge no earlier than next liquid session; use seasons as independent split units. Separate entitlement from allocation units and record ML, hectares and currency explicitly; unknown zone connectivity blocks cross-zone flow. Give identical aggregate water to two disconnected zones: optimization must retain different shadow prices; delaying trade reporting must move its first eligible feature date.

**Escalation:** missing rights, contradictory terms, unknown availability or infeasibility => BLOCKED with affected IDs and minimal reproducible evidence. Economics review uses the README ROI formula; never fill missing prices with synthetic values without explicit synthetic labeling. Human domain reviewer assesses Water rights, allocation trades and permanent entitlements are different assets. Thin reported prices, changing transfer limits and endogenous crop choice can defeat identification; crop futures may be a poor regional hedge.
