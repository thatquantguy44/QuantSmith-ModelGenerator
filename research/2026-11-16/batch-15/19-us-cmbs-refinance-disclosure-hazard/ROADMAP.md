# Roadmap — US CMBS Refinance Hazard from Public Disclosures

**Effort assumption:** One credit researcher and one structured-document engineer for 12-16 weeks. Elapsed time and ROI are conditional, not commitments.

1. **G0 — Evidence and rights (BLOCKED).** Resolve the ranked ladder in SOURCES. Obtain a timestamp/units sample for loan x public reporting vintage x maturity/outcome event; identify incomplete public coverage. STOP if required economic targets or first-public times cannot be recovered legally.
2. **G1 — Synthetic contract proof (BLOCKED on G0).** Implement the as-of envelope, public refinance cash gap, maturity hazard and amendment lineage, and independent negative fixture: Replace an original loan maturity with a later extension and verify earlier features remain unchanged; include liquidated loans and enforce waterfall conservation under default scenarios. GO only with zero future-row acceptance and explicit fallback behavior.
3. **G2 — Credible baseline (BLOCKED on G1).** Implement Logistic distress model using months-to-maturity and last publicly available DSCR with fixed rate-shock scenarios. with actual or clearly labeled scenario costs. Freeze target horizon, cohorts, primary metric and parameters before holdout. Pin dependency hashes and data manifests.
4. **G3 — Candidate challenge (BLOCKED on G2).** Compare Competing-risk survival model with refinance cash-gap covariates to baseline, with Hierarchical property-cycle hazard and cash-flow waterfall scenario optimization reserved as an optional later branch. GO if Brier improves >=10% and stress loss falls >=5% at same carry over a held-out issuance vintage; STOP if modification histories, exited loans or outcome timing are missing for >10% of sample.
5. **G4 — Capacity and shadow (BLOCKED on G3).** Verify Illustrative $50m credit exposure; position limits depend on tranche liquidity and loss subordination. No assumption that a loan risk score is directly tradable or every CMBS has a liquid hedge. At least 60 eligible decisions and 90 calendar days, or longer to include a full native seasonal/event cycle, are required before production review. Rarity-driven insufficient samples remain BLOCKED; synthetic shocks cannot count as real economic observations.
6. **G5 — Production review (BLOCKED on G4).** Reconcile competing-outcome probabilities, missing-field coverage and tranche mapping limits with a private independently maintained reference, confirm operational ownership and rollback, then request separate deployment/trading authority. Any hard-constraint breach halts the pilot.

Release the simplest model that passes all gates; abandon if measured break-even annual benefit from README cannot be justified. A statistically interesting negative result may still support publication: High: public disclosure vintages and competing refinancing paths; publication depends on reliable field extraction and sample representativeness.
