# Research-agent contract — US CMBS Refinance Hazard from Public Disclosures

**Authority:** analyze United States commercial property and securitized credit using public, rights-approved or synthetic inputs; propose experiments and auditable recommendations. This contract does not instantiate an agent executor. Do not trade, message counterparties, repricing-contracts, certify legal rights, grant data access, deploy or merge.

**Inputs:** Public ABS-EE/10-D/10-K filings, accepted timestamps, loan maturity/amendment histories, NOI/DSCR field definitions, property type, rate terms and outcome reports; licensed bond/REIT pricing and servicing histories as needed. An example filing does not establish complete coverage. Require `loan x public reporting vintage x maturity/outcome event` plus source/version/hash, unit/currency and the common time envelope. Public run mode rejects permissioned adapters and vendor extracts whose redistribution has not been approved.

**Decision scope:** Limit public CMBS/REIT credit exposure according to released refinance cash-flow gaps and competing maturity outcomes. Build public refinance cash gap, maturity hazard and amendment lineage, compare baseline `Logistic distress model using months-to-maturity and last publicly available DSCR with fixed rate-shock scenarios.` with `Competing-risk survival model with refinance cash-gap covariates`, and return competing-outcome probabilities, missing-field coverage and tranche mapping limits. Preserve failed fits and HOLD/BLOCKED actions.

**Evidence gates:** distinguish FACT (a linked source supports precisely the stated claim), HYPOTHESIS (the new research question), ASSUMPTION (cost/capacity/timing), CALCULATION (explicit inputs/formula) and UNVERIFIED. A paper or matching citation span cannot establish a new empirical effect. No statement of outperformance is permitted before [EVALS](EVALS.md) and QMG-20261116-19-AC-01 pass.

**Hard controls:** Update risk after SEC acceptance plus one business day parsing/review; a prior reporting month's NOI remains unavailable until filing. Amendments are appended and never rewrite earlier risk snapshots. Require filing accession and field-level source path; unparsed or contradictory outcome definitions fail closed for that loan. Replace an original loan maturity with a later extension and verify earlier features remain unchanged; include liquidated loans and enforce waterfall conservation under default scenarios.

**Escalation:** missing rights, contradictory terms, unknown availability or infeasibility => BLOCKED with affected IDs and minimal reproducible evidence. Economics review uses the README ROI formula; never fill missing prices with synthetic values without explicit synthetic labeling. Human domain reviewer assesses Surviving securitizations and amended files omit failed/removed loans; appraisals lag. Tranche waterfalls and selection into public deals prevent loan-level risk from mapping directly to bond P&L.
