# Research workflow — US CMBS Refinance Hazard from Public Disclosures

1. **Data => rights/time audit.** Ingest loan x public reporting vintage x maturity/outcome event through proposed `cmbs_public_vintages`; save exact release bytes privately where rights permit and only public-safe manifests here. Check Update risk after SEC acceptance plus one business day parsing/review; a prior reporting month's NOI remains unavailable until filing. Amendments are appended and never rewrite earlier risk snapshots. Unknown data rights or availability yields BLOCKED.
2. **Hypothesis => pre-registration.** Freeze A vintage-safe competing-risk model improves 12-month refinancing-distress Brier score by at least 10% versus maturity wall plus current DSCR and lowers stress portfolio loss by 5% at equal spread carry. Define native-horizon targets and independent event groups; lock costs, limits and partition dates before seeing holdout outcomes.
3. **Baseline => reproducible control.** Implement Logistic distress model using months-to-maturity and last publicly available DSCR with fixed rate-shock scenarios. Verify Replace an original loan maturity with a later extension and verify earlier features remain unchanged; include liquidated loans and enforce waterfall conservation under default scenarios. Keep equal information and economic constraints across methods.
4. **Candidate => interpretable action.** Build public refinance cash gap, maturity hazard and amendment lineage; fit/solve Competing-risk survival model with refinance cash-gap covariates. Return competing-outcome probabilities, missing-field coverage and tranche mapping limits plus a feasibility/time certificate. Advanced research `Hierarchical property-cycle hazard and cash-flow waterfall scenario optimization` stays optional.
5. **Validation => objective verdict.** Report Brier/calibration, distress precision-recall, vintage drift, stress loss, carry retained and concentration. GO if Brier improves >=10% and stress loss falls >=5% at same carry over a held-out issuance vintage; STOP if modification histories, exited loans or outcome timing are missing for >10% of sample. Double execution costs and halve capacity; include zero-benefit and delayed-launch ROI scenarios.
6. **Review => private shadow.** Domain reviewer challenges Surviving securitizations and amended files omit failed/removed loans; appraisals lag. Tranche waterfalls and selection into public deals prevent loan-level risk from mapping directly to bond P&L. Complete rights and capacity evidence before any prospective shadow run. Human review is required; runtime deployment and trading need separate authority.

All transitions are future gates, currently BLOCKED. A failed experiment is retained with source/config/code hashes; reruns cannot overwrite an adverse outcome or silently relax a threshold. Generated reports label FACT/HYPOTHESIS/ASSUMPTION/CALCULATION/UNVERIFIED and preserve the original baseline alongside candidate output.
