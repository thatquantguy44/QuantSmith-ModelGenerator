# Roadmap — Owner Co-Withdrawal Limits for Lendable Inventory

**Effort assumption:** One risk researcher and one engineer for 12 weeks, conditional on a sufficiently long approved panel. Elapsed time and ROI are conditional, not commitments.

1. **G0 — Evidence and rights (BLOCKED).** Resolve the ranked ladder in SOURCES. Obtain a timestamp/units sample for owner x security x dated available-inventory snapshot; identify incomplete public coverage. STOP if required economic targets or first-public times cannot be recovered legally.
2. **G1 — Synthetic contract proof (BLOCKED on G0).** Implement the as-of envelope, common withdrawal factor, owner lineage groups and replacement tail scenarios, and independent negative fixture: Duplicate an owner under two IDs: effective diversification must not improve; simulate perfect co-withdrawal and verify the model does not report independent-source protection. GO only with zero future-row acceptance and explicit fallback behavior.
3. **G2 — Credible baseline (BLOCKED on G1).** Implement Static 10% owner concentration cap with independent withdrawal probabilities. with actual or clearly labeled scenario costs. Freeze target horizon, cohorts, primary metric and parameters before holdout. Pin dependency hashes and data manifests.
4. **G3 — Candidate challenge (BLOCKED on G2).** Compare Hierarchical common-factor withdrawal model and CVaR allocation to baseline, with Distributionally robust copula stress allocation with mandate-group shocks reserved as an optional later branch. GO if 99th-percentile cost improves >=15%, normal net revenue loss <=2%, and at least 3 distinct withdrawal episodes are held out; STOP if dependence is unidentified or fewer than 24 months of owner histories exist.
5. **G4 — Capacity and shadow (BLOCKED on G3).** Verify Illustrative $500m supported inventory; direct benefits limited to replacement-cost savings and retained fee revenue. Extreme withdrawals may have no feasible replacement regardless of model. At least 60 eligible decisions and 90 calendar days, or longer to include a full native seasonal/event cycle, are required before production review. Rarity-driven insufficient samples remain BLOCKED; synthetic shocks cannot count as real economic observations.
6. **G5 — Production review (BLOCKED on G4).** Reconcile source concentration limits and joint-withdrawal stress certificate with a private independently maintained reference, confirm operational ownership and rollback, then request separate deployment/trading authority. Any hard-constraint breach halts the pilot.

Release the simplest model that passes all gates; abandon if measured break-even annual benefit from README cannot be justified. A statistically interesting negative result may still support publication: High if dependence is identifiable; otherwise honest bounds on inventory diversification are useful internal research.
