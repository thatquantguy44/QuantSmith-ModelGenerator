# Research workflow — Owner Co-Withdrawal Limits for Lendable Inventory

1. **Data => rights/time audit.** Ingest owner x security x dated available-inventory snapshot through proposed `owner_inventory`; save exact release bytes privately where rights permit and only public-safe manifests here. Check Set next-day inventory limits after local close using confirmed availability received by cutoff; public fund holdings enter at filing acceptance, not portfolio as-of date. Never condition on the owner's future sale or recall notice. Unknown data rights or availability yields BLOCKED.
2. **Hypothesis => pre-registration.** Freeze Tail co-withdrawal limits reduce 99th-percentile replacement cost by at least 15% versus owner-notional caps without sacrificing more than 2% normal lending revenue. Define native-horizon targets and independent event groups; lock costs, limits and partition dates before seeing holdout outcomes.
3. **Baseline => reproducible control.** Implement Static 10% owner concentration cap with independent withdrawal probabilities. Verify Duplicate an owner under two IDs: effective diversification must not improve; simulate perfect co-withdrawal and verify the model does not report independent-source protection. Keep equal information and economic constraints across methods.
4. **Candidate => interpretable action.** Build common withdrawal factor, owner lineage groups and replacement tail scenarios; fit/solve Hierarchical common-factor withdrawal model and CVaR allocation. Return source concentration limits and joint-withdrawal stress certificate plus a feasibility/time certificate. Advanced research `Distributionally robust copula stress allocation with mandate-group shocks` stays optional.
5. **Validation => objective verdict.** Report tail replacement cost, simultaneous-shortfall calibration, revenue retained, stress coverage and concentration. GO if 99th-percentile cost improves >=15%, normal net revenue loss <=2%, and at least 3 distinct withdrawal episodes are held out; STOP if dependence is unidentified or fewer than 24 months of owner histories exist. Double execution costs and halve capacity; include zero-benefit and delayed-launch ROI scenarios.
6. **Review => private shadow.** Domain reviewer challenges Mandate closure is not an iid recall; inactive and terminated owners must remain in the sample. Co-movement can be driven by security demand rather than shared owner behavior. Complete rights and capacity evidence before any prospective shadow run. Human review is required; runtime deployment and trading need separate authority.

All transitions are future gates, currently BLOCKED. A failed experiment is retained with source/config/code hashes; reruns cannot overwrite an adverse outcome or silently relax a threshold. Generated reports label FACT/HYPOTHESIS/ASSUMPTION/CALCULATION/UNVERIFIED and preserve the original baseline alongside candidate output.
