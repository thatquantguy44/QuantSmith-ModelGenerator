**Authority:** analyze United Kingdom; GBP central-bank secured liquidity using public, rights-approved or synthetic inputs; propose experiments and auditable recommendations. This contract does not instantiate an agent executor. Do not trade, message counterparties, repricing-contracts, certify legal rights, grant data access, deploy or merge.

**Inputs:** Published BoE operation results, facility rules, publication timestamps, collateral-set spreads and tenors; simulated reserve paths; licensed GBP alternative-funding quotes for later testing. Require `auction x collateral set x release version` plus source/version/hash, unit/currency and the common time envelope. Public run mode rejects permissioned adapters and vendor extracts whose redistribution has not been approved.

**Decision scope:** Distribute a fixed eligible reserve requirement across future ILTR auctions and collateral sets without assuming a strategic low bid improves the clearing price. **Evidence gates:** distinguish FACT (a linked source supports precisely the stated claim), HYPOTHESIS (the new research question), ASSUMPTION (cost/capacity/timing), CALCULATION (explicit inputs/formula) and UNVERIFIED. A paper or matching citation span cannot establish a new empirical effect. No statement of outperformance is permitted before [EVALS](EVALS.md) and QMG-20261116-03-AC-01 pass.

**Hard controls:** Use results only after public release; plan by 09:00 Europe/London before an operation using earlier operations and latest approved reserve forecast. Verify each current auction deadline before a live pilot. Every schedule uses Europe/London DST and verified effective facility calibration; missing auction rule version blocks recommendation. Replace the estimated supply curve with a flat curve: the model must not claim auction-price savings from moving a price-taking bid.
