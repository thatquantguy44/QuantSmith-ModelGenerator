# Roadmap — Sterling ILTR Auction Demand Smoothing

**Effort assumption:** One auction researcher and one engineer for 10-14 weeks; treasury and facility access reviews required. Elapsed time and ROI are conditional, not commitments.

1. **G0 — Evidence and rights (BLOCKED).** Resolve the ranked ladder in SOURCES. Obtain a timestamp/units sample for auction x collateral set x release version; identify incomplete public coverage. STOP if required economic targets or first-public times cannot be recovered legally.
2. **G1 — Synthetic contract proof (BLOCKED on G0).** Implement the as-of envelope, clearing-spread intervals, reserve coverage and maturity bucket exposure, and independent negative fixture: Replace the estimated supply curve with a flat curve: the model must not claim auction-price savings from moving a price-taking bid. GO only with zero future-row acceptance and explicit fallback behavior.
3. **G2 — Credible baseline (BLOCKED on G1).** Implement Equal weekly maturities with bids at independently approved maximum willingness-to-pay; compare a single-auction funding plan separately. with actual or clearly labeled scenario costs. Freeze target horizon, cohorts, primary metric and parameters before holdout. Pin dependency hashes and data manifests.
4. **G3 — Candidate challenge (BLOCKED on G2).** Compare Shape-constrained aggregate supply-curve estimation plus stochastic ladder optimization to baseline, with Partially identified robust auction planning over a set of rival-demand curves reserved as an optional later branch. GO if >=1 bp net annualized saving versus equal ladder in >=12 held-out operations and no stressed reserve shortfall. STOP if all plausible demand curves imply the same ladder or savings require underbidding actual willingness-to-pay.
5. **G4 — Capacity and shadow (BLOCKED on G3).** Verify Illustrative GBP500m reserve need in USD-equivalent ROI; size must be small relative to auction capacity or explicitly model own impact. Operational access, collateral prepositioning and bridge-funding limits bind. At least 60 eligible decisions and 90 calendar days, or longer to include a full native seasonal/event cycle, are required before production review. Rarity-driven insufficient samples remain BLOCKED; synthetic shocks cannot count as real economic observations.
6. **G5 — Production review (BLOCKED on G4).** Reconcile auction cash-demand ladder with allocation sensitivity bounds with a private independently maintained reference, confirm operational ownership and rollback, then request separate deployment/trading authority. Any hard-constraint breach halts the pilot.

Release the simplest model that passes all gates; abandon if measured break-even annual benefit from README cannot be justified. A statistically interesting negative result may still support publication: High identification interest, conditional on transparent partial-identification bounds rather than a falsely recovered demand curve.
