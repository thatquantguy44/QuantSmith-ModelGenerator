# Roadmap — South African Power Reliability Equity Exposure

**Effort assumption:** One EM equity researcher and data engineer for 12-16 weeks. Elapsed time and ROI are conditional, not commitments.

1. **G0 — Evidence and rights (BLOCKED).** Resolve the ranked ladder in SOURCES. Obtain a timestamp/units sample for firm x day and dated generation/outage release; identify incomplete public coverage. STOP if required economic targets or first-public times cannot be recovered legally.
2. **G1 — Synthetic contract proof (BLOCKED on G0).** Implement the as-of envelope, pre-disclosed energy intensity, backup coverage and released outage innovation, and independent negative fixture: Use a future outage series as a placebo and randomize exposure labels within sectors; the real-time pipeline must reject future features and significance should vanish under label randomization. GO only with zero future-row acceptance and explicit fallback behavior.
3. **G2 — Credible baseline (BLOCKED on G1).** Implement National load-shedding stage interacted with fixed sector averages and a market/FX hedge. with actual or clearly labeled scenario costs. Freeze target horizon, cohorts, primary metric and parameters before holdout. Pin dependency hashes and data manifests.
4. **G3 — Candidate challenge (BLOCKED on G2).** Compare Panel fixed effects with pre-period exposure interactions and event-placebo tests to baseline, with Hierarchical causal sensitivity bounds plus constrained cash-flow hedge portfolio reserved as an optional later branch. GO if downside hedge error improves >=10%, coefficient sign is stable in held-out periods and net benefit survives doubled spreads; STOP causal interpretation if pretrends or commodity/FX confounds remain.
5. **G4 — Capacity and shadow (BLOCKED on G3).** Verify Illustrative $20m hedge sleeve capped at 1% ADV with local spreads, market access and ZAR hedge costs; concentrated names can exhaust capacity rapidly. At least 60 eligible decisions and 90 calendar days, or longer to include a full native seasonal/event cycle, are required before production review. Rarity-driven insufficient samples remain BLOCKED; synthetic shocks cannot count as real economic observations.
6. **G5 — Production review (BLOCKED on G4).** Reconcile firm sensitivity bounds and neutral power-risk hedge research basket with a private independently maintained reference, confirm operational ownership and rollback, then request separate deployment/trading authority. Any hard-constraint breach halts the pilot.

Release the simplest model that passes all gates; abandon if measured break-even annual benefit from README cannot be justified. A statistically interesting negative result may still support publication: High if exposure and timing can be made public and causal limits are respected; a careful negative result is valuable.
