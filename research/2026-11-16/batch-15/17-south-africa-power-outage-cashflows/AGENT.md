# Research-agent contract — South African Power Reliability Equity Exposure

**Authority:** analyze South Africa; JSE industrials, retailers and exporters using public, rights-approved or synthetic inputs; propose experiments and auditable recommendations. This contract does not instantiate an agent executor. Do not trade, message counterparties, repricing-contracts, certify legal rights, grant data access, deploy or merge.

**Inputs:** Eskom public system/outage releases, exact publication versions, issuer public energy/backup disclosures, geography, sector, JSE returns including delistings, ZAR and commodity controls. Historical spatial outage maps are an unresolved source gate. Require `firm x day and dated generation/outage release` plus source/version/hash, unit/currency and the common time envelope. Public run mode rejects permissioned adapters and vendor extracts whose redistribution has not been approved.

**Decision scope:** Build a sector/beta-neutral equity hedge against power-supply deterioration using pre-disclosed operational exposure. Build pre-disclosed energy intensity, backup coverage and released outage innovation, compare baseline `National load-shedding stage interacted with fixed sector averages and a market/FX hedge.` with `Panel fixed effects with pre-period exposure interactions and event-placebo tests`, and return firm sensitivity bounds and neutral power-risk hedge research basket. Preserve failed fits and HOLD/BLOCKED actions.

**Evidence gates:** distinguish FACT (a linked source supports precisely the stated claim), HYPOTHESIS (the new research question), ASSUMPTION (cost/capacity/timing), CALCULATION (explicit inputs/formula) and UNVERIFIED. A paper or matching citation span cannot establish a new empirical effect. No statement of outperformance is permitted before [EVALS](EVALS.md) and QMG-20261116-17-AC-01 pass.

**Hard controls:** Update after verified Eskom publication; act next JSE session with prior-disclosed firm exposures. Never label realized electricity usage as known before earnings disclosure. Any exposure inferred from a later filing is unavailable for earlier dates; preserve source language and disclosure spans. Use a future outage series as a placebo and randomize exposure labels within sectors; the real-time pipeline must reject future features and significance should vanish under label randomization.

**Escalation:** missing rights, contradictory terms, unknown availability or infeasibility => BLOCKED with affected IDs and minimal reproducible evidence. Economics review uses the README ROI formula; never fill missing prices with synthetic values without explicit synthetic labeling. Human domain reviewer assesses Power failures can reflect economic demand and maintenance choices; common national shocks do not supply clean quasi-random variation. Firms adopting solar are selected, and disclosure histories are incomplete.
