# Research-agent contract — Stock-Loan Repricing Bands with Relationship Friction

**Authority:** analyze United States and developed-Europe equity loans using public, rights-approved or synthetic inputs; propose experiments and auditable recommendations. This contract does not instantiate an agent executor. Do not trade, message counterparties, repricing-contracts, certify legal rights, grant data access, deploy or merge.

**Inputs:** Existing-loan fee changes, proposal/acknowledgment timestamps, rejected changes, borrower exits, balances, fee benchmarks and support-message labor. A contributed fee panel alone cannot identify retention elasticity. Require `loan x repricing proposal/response event` plus source/version/hash, unit/currency and the common time envelope. Public run mode rejects permissioned adapters and vendor extracts whose redistribution has not been approved.

**Decision scope:** Choose a fee-change threshold and batching cadence for existing loans rather than repricing every observed basis-point move. Build fee gap, duration in band, response propensity and net processing cost, compare baseline `Match yesterday's observable benchmark daily, with contractual limits; also compare a fixed 10-bp band.` with `Impulse-control dynamic program with survival-calibrated borrower exits`, and return hold/reprice recommendation, approved band and counterfactual uncertainty. Preserve failed fits and HOLD/BLOCKED actions.

**Evidence gates:** distinguish FACT (a linked source supports precisely the stated claim), HYPOTHESIS (the new research question), ASSUMPTION (cost/capacity/timing), CALCULATION (explicit inputs/formula) and UNVERIFIED. A paper or matching citation span cannot establish a new empirical effect. No statement of outperformance is permitted before [EVALS](EVALS.md) and QMG-20261116-05-AC-01 pass.

**Hard controls:** Review at 10:00 local market time with benchmark releases and acknowledgments available by 09:55. Use an effective fee only after both contractual acceptance and booking; no retroactive filled proposals. No outbound fee message; policy must provide an auditable reason and retain borrower/owner rights as hard constraints. Set negotiation and exit costs to zero: the optimal inaction band should collapse under an otherwise frictionless model; rejected proposals must never become realized fees.

**Escalation:** missing rights, contradictory terms, unknown availability or infeasibility => BLOCKED with affected IDs and minimal reproducible evidence. Economics review uses the README ROI formula; never fill missing prices with synthetic values without explicit synthetic labeling. Human domain reviewer assesses Repricing is endogenous to scarcity and relationship quality. Off-policy evaluation cannot fix zero action overlap; randomized eligible shadow suggestions need business approval.
