# Research workflow — Stock-Loan Repricing Bands with Relationship Friction

1. **Data => rights/time audit.** Ingest loan x repricing proposal/response event through proposed `loan_repricing`; save exact release bytes privately where rights permit and only public-safe manifests here. Check Review at 10:00 local market time with benchmark releases and acknowledgments available by 09:55. Use an effective fee only after both contractual acceptance and booking; no retroactive filled proposals. Unknown data rights or availability yields BLOCKED.
2. **Hypothesis => pre-registration.** Freeze A two-sided repricing band increases net relationship-adjusted revenue by at least 5% versus daily benchmark matching without reducing risk-adjusted balances by more than 2%. Define native-horizon targets and independent event groups; lock costs, limits and partition dates before seeing holdout outcomes.
3. **Baseline => reproducible control.** Implement Match yesterday's observable benchmark daily, with contractual limits; also compare a fixed 10-bp band. Verify Set negotiation and exit costs to zero: the optimal inaction band should collapse under an otherwise frictionless model; rejected proposals must never become realized fees. Keep equal information and economic constraints across methods.
4. **Candidate => interpretable action.** Build fee gap, duration in band, response propensity and net processing cost; fit/solve Impulse-control dynamic program with survival-calibrated borrower exits. Return hold/reprice recommendation, approved band and counterfactual uncertainty plus a feasibility/time certificate. Advanced research `Hierarchical Bayesian partially observed control with conservative policy evaluation` stays optional.
5. **Validation => objective verdict.** Report net margin, retention-adjusted balance, messages per loan, calibration, implementation shortfall and subgroup concentration. GO if >=5% net revenue improvement with bootstrap lower bound >0 and balance loss <=2% in a pre-registered eligible cohort; no subgroup >5% deterioration. STOP if benefit relies on unobserved rejected requests or violates agreed repricing rights. Double execution costs and halve capacity; include zero-benefit and delayed-launch ROI scenarios.
6. **Review => private shadow.** Domain reviewer challenges Repricing is endogenous to scarcity and relationship quality. Off-policy evaluation cannot fix zero action overlap; randomized eligible shadow suggestions need business approval. Complete rights and capacity evidence before any prospective shadow run. Human review is required; runtime deployment and trading need separate authority.

All transitions are future gates, currently BLOCKED. A failed experiment is retained with source/config/code hashes; reruns cannot overwrite an adverse outcome or silently relax a threshold. Generated reports label FACT/HYPOTHESIS/ASSUMPTION/CALCULATION/UNVERIFIED and preserve the original baseline alongside candidate output.
