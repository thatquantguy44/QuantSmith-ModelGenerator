## Claim taxonomy

- FACT: official portal/methodology existence and exact stated scope; links above and per-idea SOURCES delimit the support.
- HYPOTHESIS: every predicted scientific/economic improvement and proposed falsification threshold.
- ASSUMPTION: budgets, launch lag, capacity, spreads, latency without recordings, effort, utility conversion and market access.
- CALCULATION: ROI arithmetic and deterministic ranking order from declared editorial scores.
- UNVERIFIED/BLOCKED: data extraction, complete timestamp history, data rights, usable sample, execution economics, package pins, empirical model, advanced runtime and production performance.
